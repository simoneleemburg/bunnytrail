- a state (where it is in εσδ-space)
- a frame orientation (how it interprets / couples to that space)

A Mundus Frame is a local basis structure in εσδ-space that determines how drift and Cardinal-fluxes are resolved into experience and trajectory. Frame orientation is the alignment of this basis relative to the local drift field.

A Mundus Frame is basically how a being “leans into reality” by default. It’s the habitual alignment between identity and εσδ-space — what aspects of existence feel “straight”, “heavy”, “real”, or “obvious” to it.

A Mundus Frame is the default stability configuration through which a being maintains coherence in εσδ-space. Different kinds of beings are not composed of different substances, but are stabilized around different baseline frame orientations and drift compatibilities.

εσδ-space admits four asymptotically stable orientations: Equilibrium and the three Cardinals. All other configurations are metastable and will, in the absence of sustained Nearing or structural constraints, drift toward one of these attractors over time. Mundus Frames are transient stabilizations within this field, defined by their relative bias toward these fundamental orientations.

Nearing is the intentional transformation of this orientation, altering how drift is decomposed without directly modifying the underlying field.

Drift is the passive trajectory of a thing through εσδ-space under the influence of local gradients and its Mundus Frame configuration. In the absence of Nearing, drift relaxes toward stable local equilibrium states; unsupported perturbations diminish as the thing settles into its local basin, or equilibrium.

Nearing is intentional reorientation of a local εσδ-frame relative to the the natural drift trajectory. Nearing requires an interaction to “push” against the εσδ-frame. We can think of this as a force of will giving a torque against the drift gradient.
Nearing results in a change in the drift vector. There is an inertia to drift, based on the object’s Identity.

Reorientations of a local εσδ-frame relative to drift produce different dynamical outcomes:

- alignment → amplification of drift trajectory
- partial divergence → curved or orbital drift paths
- orthogonal reorientation → stabilized non-basin trajectories
- complex reorientation → metastable or hovering configurations
