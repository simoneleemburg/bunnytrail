This is the bundled sample world that ships with the Bunnytrail engine.
It exists to keep the engine's dogfood loop runnable without an external
content tree, and to give new world authors a reference layout to copy.

Two clusters (Mistwood and Tideholm), a small cast of people, places,
and bunnies, and a soft pink-and-blue theme that demonstrates the
engine's per-entity link-theming hooks (see
`/guides/welcome` and `assets/theme.css`).

Replace this file in your own world repo to set your own name,
tagline, and homepage lede.
