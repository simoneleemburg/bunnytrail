Clover keeps a burrow under the hawthorn at [[willow-cottage]]'s
back fence. The grey-blue stripe down their spine is what gives
them away in the long grass — once you know to look for it,
you'll see Clover everywhere.

Friends with [[pip]], who visits at dawn.
