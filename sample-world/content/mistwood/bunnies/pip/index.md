Pip travels in [[alder]]'s coat pocket on lamp-lighting evenings.
The lantern light catches on the inside of their ears and turns
them the soft rose colour the wood always notices first.

When [[briar]] is mapping near [[stone-circle]], Pip sits very
still on the topmost stone and watches.
