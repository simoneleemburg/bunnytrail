Meren has run the Tideholm docks for sixteen seasons. The
tide-book on their desk is in its third volume; the first two
are shelved in the harbor office and consulted by every
visiting captain.

[[saffron]] sleeps on whichever volume is currently in use,
which means Meren has learnt to write around a small warm
butter-coloured weight.
