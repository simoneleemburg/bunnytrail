An Eidolon is a constructed vessel that allows a
[[beings/naya|Naya]] to manifest physically on
[[concepts/asthera|Asthera]] and serves, simultaneously, as an
exoskeleton for the human bound to that Naya. Inside an Eidolon,
human and Naya merge into a single, amplified existence — one
embodied creature with the cognition and resonance of two.

The union grants immense power. It also risks erosion, distortion,
and loss of self. Only those with a stable enough sense of identity
can endure prolonged merging without harm.

## What an Eidolon is made of

Each Eidolon is built from a material framework — wood, stone,
metal, glass, or some combination — shaped by both the Naya's
nature and the [[concepts/eidolon-knight|Eidolon Knight]]'s
identity. No two Eidolons are quite alike, because no two pairs
of Naya and human are.

At the centre of every Eidolon is a [[concepts/skyblood|Skyblood]]
core, which powers it and stabilises the merging within. Without
Skyblood, an Eidolon is inert. With it, the construct becomes
something between a body, a tool, and a partner.

## What an Eidolon does

In use, Eidolons can:

- Sustain combat and labour at intensities no unmerged human or
  Naya could reach alone.
- Sustain the pilot temporarily — no need for food or air during
  use, within limits.
- Travel between worlds, in concert with the practice of
  [[concepts/nearing|Nearing]] and the launch infrastructure of
  the [[concepts/world-pillars|World Pillars]].

Use carries cost. After even a successful merging, the pilot is
left under heavy physical and psychological strain — the longer
the use, the deeper the strain.

## Cultural variation

The Sister Worlds approach Eidolons differently. On
[[places/nebelheim|Nebelheim]], Eidolons are used pragmatically:
defence, labour, survival. On [[places/bayurinda|Bayurinda]] they
are slower, more ritualised, treated as a path of practice. On
[[places/sharazan|Sharazan]] they are elaborate, symbolic, and
public — duels and displays in Eidolon form shape social
hierarchy.

The [[concepts/eidolon-knight|Eidolon Knight]] — the human who
has bound a Naya and pilots their shared vessel — is a recognised
role across all three worlds, but the meaning of that role varies
sharply between them.
