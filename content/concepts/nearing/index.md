Nearing is a practice — or, more accurately, a family of related
practices — by which an embodied being approaches
[[concepts/nareth|Nareth]] without crossing into it. The word does
the work of the idea: one _nears_. One does not enter; one does
not remain at safe distance either. The whole point is the
threshold.

In Asthera, identity, memory, and meaning are bounded by form. In
Nareth, they run together in a fluid resonant state. A practitioner
who nears Nareth opens themselves, briefly and partially, to that
fluidity — and depending on tradition and intent, the opening can
do very different things.

## Why one nears

The major recognised purposes:

- To **perceive**: to hear the resonance of a person, a place, a
  phenomenon, and translate it into terms the material world can
  use. The oracular tradition rests on this.
- To **change**: to allow part of the self to drift and recompose
  in contact with Nareth, and to return slightly other than one
  was. The transformational practices rest on this.
- To **answer**: to respond when a [[beings/naya|Naya]] reaches
  across from the other side and the situation requires a reply.
- To **travel**: to fall forward toward Nareth in motion, using
  the geometry of the resonant layer to shorten material distance.
  The interplanetary technique rests on this.
- To **send**: to place a resonance into Nareth where another
  practitioner — sometimes in another place, sometimes in another
  time — may perceive it. Messages of this kind do not always
  arrive in the order they were sent.

## Nearing as travel

In its travel form, Nearing is how the cosmos is crossed. A pilot,
usually inside an [[concepts/eidolons|Eidolon]], lets themselves
fall forward toward Nareth in flight. The resonant layer is closer
to itself across long distances than the material layer is; a body
that nears Nareth in motion covers more ground than its raw
velocity would suggest.

The technique works. It also requires enough initial velocity that
the pilot does not stall — which is what the
[[concepts/world-pillars|World Pillars]] exist for, slingshotting
Eidolons up and outward fast enough that the falling-forward can
begin safely. Crashing into something one cannot see in time is
the central failure mode of the travel form.

## Why it is dangerous

Nearing is risky for the same reason it is useful. To open to
Nareth is to admit a layer where identity is not fixed; the
practitioner can come back fragmented, distorted, or — in extreme
cases — not come back at all, in the sense of returning a self
recognisably continuous with the self that left. The recognised
failure modes mirror those of an unstable
[[concepts/binding|binding]]: identity bleed, distortion of the
inner form, and the collapse of coherent selfhood.

Because of this, serious Nearing is rarely done alone. The
[[social/oracle-triad|Oracle Triad]], as maintained by the
[[social/sisters-of-bellona|Sisters of Bellona]], exists in large
part to provide a stabilising interpretive structure around the
practice — so that what is brought back from Nareth can be held,
witnessed, and made meaning of, rather than simply suffered.
