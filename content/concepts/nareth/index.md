Nareth is the underlying resonant layer of existence in the cosmology
of Alteria. It is not a "spirit world" in a traditional sense, but a
fundamental substrate where identity, memory, and meaning exist in a
fluid, interconnected state.

When boundaries between reality weaken — or through certain practices
like Nearing — parts of existence can drift into or interact with
Nareth. It is associated with transformation, dissolution of fixed
identity, and the emergence of alternative states of being.

Counterpart to [[concepts/asthera|Asthera]], the realm of form and
persistence.
