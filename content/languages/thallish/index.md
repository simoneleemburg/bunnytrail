Thallish [[tha]] is a language of [Alteria Cognita](/cognita)
whose words sit at the centre of the compendium's cosmological
vocabulary without the compendium yet recording who speaks them.
The two terms it has supplied to the Sister Worlds' standard
register — [[concepts/asthera|Asthera]] and
[[concepts/nareth|Nareth]] — name the two great layers of
existence, and travel through every entry that touches on the
metaphysics of the world.

How those words became standard, who carried them, and from
which Thallish-speaking people they originally come, are
questions this compendium does not yet answer. There is material
on Thallish in the user's working notes that has not been
imported here. Until that work is done, the entry is a
**placeholder**: enough to let the cosmological terms be tagged
honestly, enough to record the gap, no more.

_(The home of Thallish, the people who speak it, the world it
belongs to, the relationship between Thallish and the other
languages of Cognita, and the route by which \_Asthera_ and
_Nareth_ became universal cosmological terms when most other
Thallish vocabulary did not, are not yet recorded here.)\_

## Thallish words in this compendium

The compendium uses two Thallish words as base vocabulary,
marked where they appear with the small superscript _tha_:

- **Asthera** [[tha]] — the material, embodied world-layer of
  existence. See [[concepts/asthera|Asthera]].
- **Nareth** [[tha]] — the resonant substrate that is its
  counterpart. See [[concepts/nareth|Nareth]].

Both are used so often, across so many entries, that they have
ceased to read as foreign vocabulary at all. They are simply the
words the compendium uses for the things they name.

_(Other Thallish words may yet appear when the unimported
material is brought in. They are not marked here in advance.)_
