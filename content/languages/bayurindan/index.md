Bayurindan [[bay]] is the working tongue of the archipelago
people of [[places/bayurinda|Bayurinda]] — the language spoken,
written, and sung on the islands and in the sea-caves below
them, and the language from which the world itself takes its
name of record.

It is not the [[languages/old-tongue|Old Tongue]]. The Old
Tongue is a ritual register, built for the Naya, kept in
inscriptional and recited forms, and used across the Sister
Worlds wherever the work of approximating Naya names is done.
Bayurindan is the ordinary speech of an ordinary working life:
used for the day's catch, for who is welcome in which cave, for
the songs that mark the storm seasons, and for everything the
archipelago does when it is not addressing the Naya.

## How it sounds

Bayurindan is **soft, open, and carrying**. Most syllables are
vowel-final; consonant clusters are rare, and where they occur
they tend toward palatal-nasal onsets — _ng-_, _ny-_, _ly-_ —
that sit forward in the mouth and slide rather than land. The
vowels are long and the prosody is fluent. Spoken speech can be
heard across open water at distances that would defeat a
harder-consonanted language; sung speech carries further still.

A speaker of [[languages/tholingian|Tholingian]] meeting
Bayurindan for the first time generally describes it as
_singing_. A speaker of Bayurindan meeting Tholingian for the
first time generally describes it as _shouting_. Both
descriptions are unfair to the other tongue, and both contain a
truth about it.

## Bayurindan words in this compendium

The compendium uses several Bayurindan words as base
vocabulary, marked where they first appear with the small
superscript _bay_:

- **Naya** [[bay]] — the name, in Bayurindan, of the
  [[beings/naya|primordial beings of Nareth]]. It is the
  archipelago people's word and is used throughout the
  compendium because it is the word the order learned and
  carried outward. Whether the lost surface civilisation called
  them something else, whether the deep have their own term,
  and what that term might be, are not recorded here.

- **Ngonawan Ilyardi** [[bay]] — the personal name of the
  Pilgrim of the [[social/oracle-triad|Oracle Triad]] in her
  embodied life before the Triad. A Bayurindan name with the
  language's characteristic shape: _ng-_ onset, fluent vowels,
  the palatal _-ly-_ medial. (See [[characters/ngonawan-ilyardi|her
entry]].)

_(Other Bayurindan words and names in the compendium are not
yet marked. Sweep deferred.)_

## Sibling tongues

Bayurindan is the only language of Bayurinda with an entry in
this compendium. It is not the only language of Bayurinda. What
the deep speak among themselves is not recorded here, and may
not be recordable in any form a surface mouth can hold; what
the lost surface civilisation spoke is largely lost with them,
though [the inscriptions](/languages/old-tongue#how-it-survived--partly)
preserve fragments of what was written, mostly in the
specifically ritual register that became the Old Tongue.

_(The question of how Bayurindan relates to the working tongues
of [[places/nebelheim|Nebelheim]] and
[[places/sharazan|Sharazan]], and whether any of the Sister
Worlds' languages share deep ancestry, is not yet recorded
here.)_
