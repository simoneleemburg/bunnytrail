The Mythic Age is the deepest layer of Alteria's memory.

What is held in it is held only in the form of stories. Where
the [[history/pre-recorded|Pre-recorded Age]] leaves its trace
in stones and ruins and the long custodianship of names, the
Mythic Age leaves its trace only in the telling. The
[[kinds/naya|Naya]] are spoken of as belonging to it; the
[[places/realms/asthera|realms]] are spoken of as belonging to
it; the older patterns of the world — its forces, its first
shapes — are placed by the present in the Mythic Age because
that is the only place a tradition has to put them.

Whether the Mythic Age is a _period of time_ in the same sense
as the ages that follow it, or whether it is the layer the
present uses for what cannot be otherwise placed, is itself a
question that travels from culture to culture without resolving.
