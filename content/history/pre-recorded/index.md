The Pre-recorded Age is the stretch of Alteria's history that
happened but was not kept.

It is older than the [[history/recorded|Recorded Age]] and
younger than the [[history/mythic|Mythic Age]] — long enough
ago that the present has no written memory of it, recent enough
that what is found from it (ruins, inscriptions, materials, the
deep's preserved stones) is unmistakably the work of people who
lived ordinary lives in the world the present still walks
through.

Most of what reaches the present from this age does so by
indirection: through the
[[culture/languages/old-tongue|Old Tongue]] inscriptions on
the floor of [[nuunlau|Nuunlau]], through the long
custodianship of the [[culture/languages/deep-speech|Nuunlaumud]],
through ruins on the [[bayurinda-archipelago|archipelago]]
that ought not, by any present account, to be where they are.
The drowned surface civilisation of [[bayurinda|Bayurinda]]
lived here.
