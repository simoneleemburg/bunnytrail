# The Question of Truth

There are those who say I strayed from the path.  
Perhaps. Perhaps I did.

But tell me, who seeks the root and is not burned?  
Who names the first truth and lives?

I walked beneath Pholtus' gaze, across stone, across silence, across sand. His light bore me onward. Still, the questions gathered like ash in the mouth.

Whence came the gods?  
What was the world before time?  
What shaped good and ill, what voice gave them form?  
If we forget them, do they forget us in kind?

These are my accounts.

— Rathiel, the Hermit-Priest
