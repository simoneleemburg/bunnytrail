Skyblood is a rare celestial material left behind by an
ancient shattering — a breaking older than the
[[culture/knights-of-bellona|Knights of Bellona]], older than
the worlds' current arrangement of themselves, older than most
of what is written down. What exactly was shattered is the
kind of question every long-lived tradition in
[Cognita](/cognita) has answered for itself: by long tradition
on more than one Sister World it was a god, and the phrase
_the shattered god_ is in wide ritual and scholarly use, but
the precise identity attributed to that god — and indeed
whether _god_ is the right word at all — varies between
traditions. What is not disputed is the shattering itself, and
what it left.

What Skyblood does, on the other hand, is not a matter of
tradition.

## What it does

Skyblood is, in itself, inert with respect to ordinary
geometry. It does not rotate the
[[mundus-frame|place-frame]] of the region around itself; it
does not produce a Source-tilted field; it does not radiate.
Held in the hand, it is matter.

Its property is narrower and stranger: it can **trap a
Source-tilted nature-frame**. A [[kinds/naya|Naya]], whose
nature-frame places its local
[[equilibrium|Equilibrium]] at the [[source|Source]] and which
in ordinary matter would drift back to
[[realms/nareth|Nareth]], can be held by Skyblood at the point
of contact. The trap is the material's affinity for the kind
of nature-frame a Naya carries; the holding only works for
beings whose Equilibrium sits where Skyblood's affinity does,
which is at the Source.

The coupling is by touch. Skyblood is not a field. Nothing
about it extends outward through the region around the
material; what it does, it does at the surface where it meets
something else.

This single property is what every high-order practice in the
Aurethian cluster turns on, because everything else flows from
the Naya, not from the Skyblood.

## What the Naya does, once held

A Naya trapped in a Skyblood core is anchored to a specific
physical location. From that anchor it can
[[aurethia/fabric/nearing|Near]] in directions it could not
Near from Nareth: toward the
[[culture/eidolon-knight|Knight]] who is in physical contact
with the vessel, toward the form of the
[[kinds/eidolon|Eidolon]] itself, toward the region of space
around the vessel.

The Naya's outward Nearing through the vessel is what makes
the Eidolon a vessel rather than a hollow construct. The
Knight does not pilot a Skyblood-cored frame; the Knight
pilots a vessel through which a Naya is leaning out. The
local place-frame distortion that an active Eidolon shows in
its surrounding region — the small Source-ward tilt observable
near a working construct — is the Naya's outward Nearing, not
the Skyblood's own field. Skyblood has no field.

This is also why an Eidolon at rest is _sleeping_. A Naya at
rest in its anchor is not Nearing outward. The vessel
produces nothing.

## What Skyblood does for the Knight

By holding the Naya at a fixed and reachable point, Skyblood
makes the Knight's [[aurethia/fabric/nearing|Nearing]] toward
the Naya a Nearing toward a particular place — and the Naya,
from its anchor, can Near toward the Knight in return. The
two parties meet partway. What the practice calls Skyblood's
_anchoring of identity during merging_ is this: not that
Skyblood holds the Knight against dissolution, but that it
gives the Knight a specifically-located being to reach toward,
who is also reaching back. The Knight's Nearing is shorter
because the Naya is doing some of the work from the other
side.

The Knight's own coherence — their hold on themselves during
the merging — is the Knight's, not the Skyblood's. Skyblood
does not anchor a Knight in interplanetary transit either.
The Knight's _anchor_ — the holding of their own frame against
the Void's place-frame in the long passages between
[[kinds/world-pillar|Pillars]] — is the Knight's own work,
and is treated in the [[aurethia/fabric/nearing|Nearing]]
entry.

## What it does not do

Skyblood reinforces what is already present. It does not
**create** identity, and it does not anchor a Knight against
their own depths. A fragile self breaks under merging; a rigid
self shatters; an adaptive self holds. The Naya meeting the
Knight partway makes the merging more survivable, but the
Knight still has to make the Nearing, and the self that does
it must be capable of doing it. Skyblood does not change which
of those a person is.

This is one of the cosmology's harder facts, and one of the
Knights of Bellona's central teachings.

## Politics

Skyblood is sacred, scarce, and politically controlled. Who
owns the deposits, who is permitted to refine it, and who is
permitted to use it — and how much — are questions the worlds
answer differently.
