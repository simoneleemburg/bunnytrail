Skyblood is a rare celestial material left behind by an
ancient shattering — a breaking older than the
[[culture/knights-of-bellona|Knights of Bellona]], older than
the worlds' current arrangement of themselves, older than most
of what is written down. What exactly was shattered is the
kind of question every long-lived tradition in
[Cognita](/cognita) has answered for itself: by long tradition
on more than one Sister World it was a god, and the phrase
_the shattered god_ is in wide ritual and scholarly use, but
the precise identity attributed to that god — and indeed
whether _god_ is the right word at all — varies between
traditions. What is not disputed is the shattering itself, and
what it left.

What Skyblood does, on the other hand, is not a matter of
tradition.

## What it does

In foundation terms, Skyblood is the only material the cluster
has so far identified that can hold a Source-rotated
[[mundus-frame|place-frame]] in ordinary matter. Inside a
region of Skyblood — or more typically, around a refined
Skyblood core — local [[equilibrium|Equilibrium]] sits at the
[[source|Source]] rather than at the εσδ-centroid. The
rotation is the material's own property; nothing has to be
holding it for it to be there.

Almost every high-order practice in the Alteria cosmology
turns on this single fact. Three consequences carry most of
the load:

- It is what makes the [[binding|binding]] of a
  [[kinds/naya|Naya]] into an [[kinds/eidolon|Eidolon]]
  possible. The Naya's nature-frame places its local
  Equilibrium at the Source; in the Source-rotated interior
  of a Skyblood-cored vessel, the Naya is at rest. Without
  Skyblood, no construct of ordinary matter can produce a
  place-frame the Naya can rest in.
- It is what makes piloting an Eidolon survivable for a
  [[culture/eidolon-knight|Knight]]. Piloting is a sustained
  [[aurethia/fabric/nearing|Nearing]] toward the Naya inside
  the vessel, and deep Nearing dissolves the practitioner. In
  the Source-rotated interior the Naya is already close to
  the Knight in εσδ-space: local Equilibrium is at the Source,
  and the Naya is there. The Knight reaches the Naya by
  leaning a little, not by leaning all the way. Less leaning
  is less dissolution. This is what the practice calls
  Skyblood's _anchoring of identity during merging_: it does
  not hold the Knight against the Nearing; it shortens the
  Nearing the Knight has to do.
- It is what makes the Eidolon a vessel at all rather than
  inert material. The vessel's other parts — armour,
  articulation, [[fabric/materials/urouch|urouch]] hide,
  [[fabric/materials/vochran|vochran]] frame — are built
  around the core, and what they are built around is the
  rotated region the core produces.

## What it does not do

Skyblood reinforces what is already present. It does not
**create** identity, and it does not anchor a Knight against
their own depths. A fragile self breaks under merging; a rigid
self shatters; an adaptive self holds. The rotated frame
shortens the Nearing, but the Nearing still happens, and the
self that does it must be capable of doing it. Skyblood does
not change which of those a person is.

This is one of the cosmology's harder facts, and one of the
Knights of Bellona's central teachings.

Skyblood also does not anchor a Knight in interplanetary
transit. The Knight's _anchor_ — the holding of their own frame
against the Void's place-frame in the long passages between
[[kinds/world-pillar|Pillars]] — is the Knight's own work, and
is treated in the [[aurethia/fabric/nearing|Nearing]] entry.
Skyblood provides the working-region within which the Knight
anchors; the anchoring itself is the Knight's.

## Politics

Skyblood is sacred, scarce, and politically controlled. Who
owns the deposits, who is permitted to refine it, and who is
permitted to use it — and how much — are questions the worlds
answer differently.
