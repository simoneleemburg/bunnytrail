The Recorded Age is the age in which the Sister Worlds keep
written memory of themselves.

The [[culture/knights-of-bellona|Knights of Bellona]]'s
rosters reach back through it. The [[culture/oracle-triad|Oracle
Triad]] has held its seat through much of it. The institutions,
the working tongues, and the practices of present life took
the shape they now wear within it.

The boundary between the Recorded Age and the
[[history/pre-recorded|Pre-recorded Age]] is not a line. It is
the slow thinning-out of testimony: the further back one
reaches, the fewer hands held the record, until what remains
is a few inscriptions and a long silence.
