Rathiel signs himself the Hermit-Priest. He was born to Pelor, he
says, and bathed in his radiance and served at his altar; he also
walked beneath Pholtus' gaze, and judged that the two names are
one god seen from East and West. By his own account he walked far
enough to lose the path he was raised in.

The account survives as five fragments — the [[artefacts/bonedust-scrolls|Bonedust Scrolls]] —
recovered from a sealed candle-crypt beneath the Temple of the
Dawnborn in Solinthra. They are not sermons. They are the
questions of a priest who could not stop asking, written for
whoever might come after and ask them too.
