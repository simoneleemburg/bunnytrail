The Hollow Companion is the visible star of the
[[hollow-binary|Hollow Binary]] — the body
that can be seen, observed to orbit something the eye cannot
resolve. That something is the
[[the-dark-companion|Dark Companion]].

What the visible star is, in itself, is a star — in working
scholarly catalogues, one that does not move as a single
star moves. The account of what the Hollow Binary's
arrangement does to [[nearing|Nearing]] lives on the
[[the-dark-companion|Dark Companion]] entry.

"The Hollow Companion" is the standard scholarly counterpart
to "the Dark Companion."
