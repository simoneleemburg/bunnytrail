Asthera [[tha]] is the material, embodied world-layer of existence in Alteria.
It represents stable form, continuity, and physical reality — where
identities persist as distinct beings, and where worlds, civilizations,
and histories unfold in tangible, structured time.

In foundation terms, Asthera is the Aurethian name for the broad
central region of [[mundus|Mundus]] — the part of the interior
whose [[mundus-frame|Mundus Frame]] is the trivial one, with
local [[equilibrium|Equilibrium]] at the εσδ-centroid. Most
things spend most of their time here, leaning a little but not
hard, because for things with the trivial frame this is where
the basin is. The other Aurethian realms are not separate from
Mundus; they are the regions of Mundus whose place-frames are
rotated toward one Cardinal or another.

It is the realm of form and persistence, in contrast to the fluid,
resonant nature of [[realms/nareth|Nareth]].

## On the name

The word _Asthera_ is [[culture/languages/thallish|Thallish]] —
one of two cosmological terms the Sister Worlds have, almost
universally, adopted from that language for the great layers of
existence (the other is [[realms/nareth|Nareth]]).
