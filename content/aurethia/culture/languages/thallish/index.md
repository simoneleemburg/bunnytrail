Thallish [[tha]] is a language of [Alteria Cognita](/cognita)
whose words have travelled further than its speakers. Two of its
terms — [[realms/asthera|Asthera]] and
[[realms/nareth|Nareth]] — have become the standard names across
the Sister Worlds for the two great layers of existence, and
travel through every register that touches on the metaphysics of
the world.

## Thallish words in use

Two Thallish words are used as standard vocabulary across the
Sister Worlds, marked where they appear with the small
superscript _tha_:

- **Asthera** [[tha]] — the material, embodied world-layer of
  existence. See [[realms/asthera|Asthera]].
- **Nareth** [[tha]] — the resonant substrate that is its
  counterpart. See [[realms/nareth|Nareth]].

Both are used so often, across so many entries, that they have
ceased to read as foreign vocabulary. They are simply the words
for the things they name.
