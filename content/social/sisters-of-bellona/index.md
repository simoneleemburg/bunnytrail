The Sisters of Bellona are the order charged with maintaining the
[[social/oracle-triad|Oracle Triad]] and, through it, the balance
between [[concepts/asthera|Asthera]] and [[concepts/nareth|Nareth]].
They are the institutional memory and the institutional discipline
around [[concepts/nearing|Nearing]]: the body that decides what is
sound practice, who is ready to near, and how the meaning brought
back is held.

The order takes its name from Bellona, a figure whose history is
recorded elsewhere in their archives — a founder, by tradition,
though the lineage is older than any single founding.

## What they do

The Sisters' central responsibility is the continuous presence of
three serving Oracles — one each for Interpretation, Endurance, and
Witnessing. The work of finding, recognising, and supporting the
right being in each role is the slow backbone of the order's life;
the work of stabilising what those Oracles bring back is its daily
practice.

Around this core, the Sisters keep the auxiliary disciplines:
records of past Nearings and what they yielded, training for
practitioners who will never serve as Oracles but who near in
support of other work, and the diplomatic relationships through
which the Triad's interpretations reach the wider world.

## Membership

Membership is not restricted by species, by gender, or by origin —
fitting an order whose central system is held by three beings drawn
from three different modes of existence. The Sisters' inclusiveness
is itself doctrinal: a Triad of any narrower composition would, by
their reasoning, be unable to do what the Triad is for.
