The Oracle Triad is a sacred threefold system maintained by the
[[social/sisters-of-bellona|Sisters of Bellona]]. Its purpose is to
hold the balance between [[concepts/asthera|Asthera]], the material
plane, and [[concepts/nareth|Nareth]], the resonant substrate — and,
more practically, to provide a stabilising interpretive structure
for [[concepts/nearing|Nearing]], so that the meeting of form and
resonance does not collapse into distortion.

The Triad is not a hierarchy. It is three roles, each indispensable,
each speaking from a different mode of existence and perception.
Together they form a triangle in which any one role unsupported by
the others becomes dangerous: pure interpretation runs to delusion,
pure endurance to ossification, pure witnessing to dissolution.

## The three Oracles

**Interpretation — the extraction of meaning from signal.** Held by
[[characters/ngonawan-ilyardi|Ngonawan Ilyardi]], the Sacred Serpent
of Resonance. The Oracle of Interpretation translates the shifting
phenomena of resonance into coherent understanding, turning unstable
signal into structure that the material world can act on.

**Endurance — continuity carried through time.** Held by
[[characters/sabina-vaarn|Sabina Vaarn]], the Pilgrim of Ages. The
Oracle of Endurance anchors interpretation in lived historical
flow. What Interpretation makes legible, Endurance makes durable —
holding meaning across generations so that transient insight
becomes something a tradition can carry.

**Witnessing — recursive patterns observed across recurrence.**
Held by [[characters/uraya-ur|Uraya Ur]], the Watcher of Spiraling
Echoes. The Oracle of Witnessing perceives meaning not as linear
narrative but as spiraling repetition — patterns visible only
across collapse and re-emergence. Witnessing is what keeps
Interpretation honest and Endurance from mistaking persistence for
truth.

## Why three

The Triad's structure mirrors the cosmology it tends. Interpretation
is an Astheran act — extracting form from the resonant. Witnessing
is a Naretic act — perceiving across patterns that never settle into
form. Endurance is the bridge: the mortal carrying of meaning
through time, the work that only embodied life can do. Each Oracle
embodies, by lineage and by the shape of their own becoming, the
mode of existence they speak from.
