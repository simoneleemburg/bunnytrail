The Hollow Binary is the scholarly name for the binary system
that lies near to, but apart from, the
[[cosmology/systems/sister-system|Sister System]]. Its two
bodies are a visible companion star and the
[[cosmology/black-holes/the-dark-companion|Dark Companion]] — an invisible
second body resolvable only by what it does to its companion.

The system's name across the Sister Worlds is well-attested.
The individual bodies are named in many traditions; the
arrangement as a whole is, in working scholarly use,
_the Hollow Binary_.

What is known about the system as a whole — its orbital
period, its distance, the question of whether anything else
travels with it — sits in the entries for its two bodies. The
Hollow Binary is the name for the arrangement; what the
arrangement is, is the question its bodies open.
