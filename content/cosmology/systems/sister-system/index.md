The Sister System is the stellar arrangement the three Sister
Worlds share. [[places/bayurinda|Bayurinda]],
[[places/nebelheim|Nebelheim]], and [[places/sharazan|Sharazan]]
all orbit the same star; the system is the name the compendium
gives to the whole — the star, its worlds, and whatever else
travels gravitationally with them.

_(The system's canonical name in any in-world scholarly use is
not yet recorded here. "The Sister System" is the compendium's
working term, derived from the existing "Sister Worlds"
register, and may not be what the worlds themselves call their
arrangement.)_

_(The bodies beyond the three known worlds — outer planets,
asteroidal material, anything the order's charts mark but the
working compendium has not yet entered — are not yet recorded
here.)_
