Aureth is the star at the centre of the
[[cosmology/systems/aureth-system|Aureth System]]. Bayurinda,
Nebelheim, and Sharazan all orbit within its light; the day
on each is the day Aureth defines for it.

_**Aureth** is Thallish — like_ Asthera _and_ Nareth, _it sits in
the compendium's standard cosmological vocabulary and is used as
the universal scholarly name. Vernacular names for the same star
in local registers may exist; they are not yet recorded here._

_Aureth's class, age, and any further catalogue of its names are
not yet recorded here._
