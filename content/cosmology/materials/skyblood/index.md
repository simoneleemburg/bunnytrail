Skyblood is a rare celestial material left behind by an ancient
shattering — a breaking older than the [[culture/knights-of-bellona|
Knights of Bellona]], older than the worlds' current arrangement
of themselves, older than most of what is written down. What
exactly was shattered is the kind of question every long-lived
tradition in [Cognita](/cognita) has answered for itself: by long
tradition on more than one Sister World it was a god, and the
phrase _the shattered god_ is in wide ritual and scholarly use,
but the precise identity attributed to that god — and indeed
whether _god_ is the right word at all — varies between
traditions and is not settled here. What is not disputed is the
shattering itself, and what it left.

What Skyblood does, on the other hand, is not a matter of
tradition. It is the material substrate of almost every
high-order practice in the Alteria cosmology — without it, the
delicate interface between [[realms/asthera|Asthera]] and
[[realms/nareth|Nareth]] cannot be held stably enough to be of
use.

## What it does

Skyblood does three related things:

- It **stabilises** [[cosmology/binding|binding]] between humans and
  [[beings/naya|Naya]] — the pact at the core of the cosmology's
  meaningful encounters.
- It **anchors identity** during the merging at the heart of an
  [[cosmology/eidolons|Eidolon]], holding the human self coherent
  while it shares form with a Naya.
- It **powers and regulates** Eidolons themselves: every Eidolon
  has a Skyblood core, without which it is inert material.

Without Skyblood, binding becomes unstable, addictive, or fatal.

## What it does not do

Skyblood reinforces what is already present. It cannot **create**
identity. A fragile self breaks under merging; a rigid self
shatters; an adaptive self holds. Skyblood does not change which
of those a person is.

This is one of the cosmology's harder facts, and one of the
Knights of Bellona's central teachings.

## Politics

Skyblood is sacred, scarce, and politically controlled. Who owns
the deposits, who is permitted to refine it, and who is permitted
to use it — and how much — are questions the worlds answer
differently.
