Aureth is the star at the centre of the
[[cosmology/systems/sister-system|Sister System]]. Bayurinda,
Nebelheim, and Sharazan all orbit within its light; the day
on each is the day Aureth defines for it.

_The name **Aureth** is Thallish. Each of the three worlds
plausibly has its own name for the same star; those are not
yet recorded here._

_Aureth's class, age, and the catalogue of its names across
the Sister Worlds are not yet recorded here._
