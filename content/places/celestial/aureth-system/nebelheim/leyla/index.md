Leyla [[tho]] is the moon of [[nebelheim|Nebelheim]] —
itself molten and volcanic, a second furnace in the sky and, by
many accounts, harder to ignore than any star.

## On the name

_Leyla_ is a [[culture/languages/tholingian|Tholingian]] word —
the name the people of [[tholingia|Tholingia]] use for the moon
that hangs over their world. It has become the name of record
across the Sister Worlds for the same reason _Nebelheim_ has:
Tholingia is the region whose working language has travelled
furthest along the routes between worlds.
