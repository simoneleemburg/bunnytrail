Nebelheim [[tho]] is the world of ash and tectonic pressure. Its sky is
shaped less by weather than by volcanic dust and mineral haze —
amber light, low and constant. Seismic instability is ordinary
life. Civilisations here are geothermal: built into calderas, along
glowing fissures, beside sulfuric fog valleys and black beaches.

A single moon, [[leyla|Leyla]] [[tho]], hangs over the
world — itself molten and volcanic, a second furnace in the sky,
harder to ignore than any star.

## On the name

_Nebelheim_ is a [[culture/languages/tholingian|Tholingian]]
word — the name the people of [[tholingia|Tholingia]] use for
their own world. It has become the name of record in the
[[culture/knights-of-bellona|Knights of Bellona]]'s rosters,
and the name in widest use across the Sister Worlds, because
Tholingia is the region the order most often deals with, and
because Tholingia is the region whose language has travelled
furthest along the routes between worlds (see
[[tholingia|Tholingia]] for why). Other regions of the planet
have their own names for it.

The adjective _Nebelheimic_, used for the planet's culture and
tone, is similarly Tholingian-derived, and similarly partial.
What is called "Nebelheimic culture" is, in fairness, mostly
what travels along Tholingian-marked routes.

## Culture and tone

Nebelheimic culture is collectivist, resilient, and duty-driven —
shaped by the planet's habit of moving under its inhabitants. The
tone of the world is survival, pressure, and discipline.

Stability is understood as temporary. People build for adaptability
rather than permanence; rituals around seismic prediction are part
of ordinary life; the planet is sometimes spoken of as if it were
breathing or molting.

## Binding on Nebelheim

[[realms/nareth|Nareth]]-touched practice on Nebelheim is regulated
and restricted by the [[culture/knights-of-bellona|Knights of
Bellona]], whose order is itself a Nebelheimic institution.
[[culture/eidolon-knight|Knighthood]] here is understood as
sacrifice and responsibility — closer to public duty than to
private path. [[eidolons|Eidolons]] are used
pragmatically: for defence, labour, and survival.
