Tholingia is a region of [[places/nebelheim|Nebelheim]] — one
named territory among many on a world whose geography refuses
to be a single country. The land is Nebelheimic in temperament:
geothermal, weather-marked, built against pressure rather than
in defiance of it.

It is the place of origin of the [[languages/tholingian|Tholingian]]
language, and the home country of two of the compendium's
characters — [[characters/freya|Freya]] and
[[characters/sabina-vaarn|Sabina Vaarn]]. Beyond those facts the
entry does not yet say much. Tholingia is named here because
without a name for the place, the language has no anchor and
the people have no home to be from.
