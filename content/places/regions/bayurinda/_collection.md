Bayurinda's regions are split by depth, not distance.
The [[bayurinda-archipelago|archipelago]] above the water and
[[nuunlau|Nuunlau]] below it are the two halves of one world,
each with its own people, its own working tongue, and its own
relationship to the [drowning](/places/celestial/aureth-system/bayurinda#the-drowning)
that shaped the present shape of both.
