Nebelheim's continents are kept apart by what lies between
them: seas in some places, lava streams in others, and
broad polar and tundra belts at the latitudes where neither
ocean nor flow has the upper hand.

The geography of calderas,
fissures and ash-plains has broken the world up into territories,
each with its own people and history.
Many of them still undocumented and unchartered.
