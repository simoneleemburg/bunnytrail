Asthera [[tha]] is the material, embodied world-layer of existence in Alteria.
It represents stable form, continuity, and physical reality — where
identities persist as distinct beings, and where worlds, civilizations,
and histories unfold in tangible, structured time.

It is the realm of form and persistence, in contrast to the fluid,
resonant nature of [[realms/nareth|Nareth]].

## On the name

The word _Asthera_ is [[culture/languages/thallish|Thallish]] — one of two
cosmological terms that the Sister Worlds have, almost universally,
adopted from that language for the great layers of existence (the
other is [[realms/nareth|Nareth]]). The route by which _Asthera_
became the standard term across [Cognita](/cognita), the people who
originally coined it, and the world Thallish is spoken on, are not
yet recorded in this compendium. (See [[culture/languages/thallish|Thallish]]
for the gap-note.)

What is recorded is the convention: when the compendium says
_Asthera_, it means what Thallish means when it says _Asthera_, and
what every Sister World now means when it borrows the word.
