Leyla [[tho]] is the moon of [[nebelheim|Nebelheim]] —
itself molten and volcanic, a second furnace in the sky and, by
many accounts, harder to ignore than any star.

## On the name

_Leyla_ is a [[culture/languages/tholingian|Tholingian]] word — the name
the people of [[tholingia|Tholingia]] use for the moon
that hangs over their world. It has become the name of record in
the compendium for the same reason _Nebelheim_ has: Tholingia is
the region whose working language has travelled furthest along
the routes between worlds. Other regions of Nebelheim presumably
have their own names for the moon. None of those are yet recorded
here.

_(What Leyla means in Tholingian — whether it is a person's name,
a descriptive word, or older than either reading — is not yet
recorded here.)_
