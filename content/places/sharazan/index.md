Sharazan is not one world but many. Some catastrophe — older than any
of its peoples' memory — broke it into a slow-tumbling cluster of
fragments, each holding a sliver of atmosphere, a span of ground, a
piece of sky. No fragment is large enough to forget the others.

What binds them is the pilgrimage routes: ancient transit-ways between
the pieces, walked and rewalked by the saints, whose stations and small
acts of repair are the only reason the routes still hold. To travel
Sharazan is to travel by faith — quite literally. The saints' presence
keeps the way open. Where they fail, the routes go dark, and a fragment
goes silent for a generation or forever.
