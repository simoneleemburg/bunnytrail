Nebelheim's surface is never quite still. New vents open in a season,
old ones seal under fresh basalt; whole valleys hiss with steam for
decades and then go cold. The maps here are dated rather than named,
and most are obsolete by the time they're drawn.

What endures, endures by moving. Cities walk — slowly, on tradition and
salvage — between the calmer plates. The wealth of a Nebelheim house is
counted in how many migrations its archives have survived.
