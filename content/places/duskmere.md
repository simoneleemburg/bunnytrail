# Duskmere

Duskmere is the last named town before the salt road dissolves into the
border-marches of the Third Veil. It is built low, in three uneven terraces
that follow the slope down to the old harbour, and the rooftops are slate the
colour of bruised cloud.

The town keeps a watch-bell on each terrace. Tradition holds that all three
once rang the night [[characters/kael-of-the-third-veil|Kael]] was born, though
the records of the border-watch are silent on the matter.

## Trade

Duskmere lives on salt, smoked fish, and the slow trade in things that cross
the Veil. Its market opens at dusk and closes when the third bell sounds.
