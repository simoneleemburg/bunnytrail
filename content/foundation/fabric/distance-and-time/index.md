The clusters of Alteria are separated by distances at which the
question "when, exactly, did that happen?" stops having a single
answer. There is no universe-wide clock the records of one cluster
could be set against the records of another; the physics of
distance forbid it. An event in one cluster and an event in
another may be related causally, or not at all, but they are
never simply _simultaneous_.

Layered on top of this — and the older record, in Aurethia at
least — is [[aurethia/fabric/nearing|Nearing]], the only practice
that has ever been recorded to cross such distances. Nearing
loosens linear time alongside spatial distance and bodily form.
Whispers heard in [[aurethia/places/realms/nareth|Nareth]] do not
behave as if the past has finished being there, and some of them
are reported to come from ahead rather than from behind. A
practitioner deep enough into Nearing is, in a sense, no longer at
any one point on a timeline; they are tilted obliquely to it.

Read together, the two layers say the same thing twice. There is
no shared _when_ relating one cluster to another — neither in the
cold accounting of distance and signal, nor in the warmer
accounting of an Eidolon Knight's traversal. Cross-cluster claims
about timing — "this happened during their such-and-such age,"
"that arrived before our recorded history began" — are claims
about what an [[kinds/account|account]] records, not about an
objective shared moment the universe itself acknowledges.

Aurethia's records of long memory — its four named ages — are an
Aurethia-frame label for Aurethian time. Other clusters, if they
ever acquire named ages, will name their own.
