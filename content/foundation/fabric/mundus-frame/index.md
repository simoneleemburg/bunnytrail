The εσδ geometry of [[mundus|Mundus]] is universal and fixed.
The three [[kinds/cardinal|Cardinals]] sit at the corners,
the three [[kinds/horizon|Horizons]] run along the edges, and
the [[equilibrium|Equilibrium]] lies at the centroid where
ε = σ = δ. The basin always has its low point at Equilibrium;
the bounds are always asymptotically far away. This is the
same everywhere, for everything.

What varies is the **map** from a thing's local experience of
Mundus into that geometry. The Mundus Frame is that map. It
fixes which point in εσδ-space counts as the thing's local
Equilibrium — the direction it drifts when nothing else is
acting on it.

## The trivial frame

For most things in most places, the Mundus Frame is the
trivial one: local Equilibrium coincides with the εσδ-centroid.
Ordinary mortals in ordinary places experience "downhill" as
"toward ε = σ = δ." They drift to the centre because the
centre is, for them, the low point.

This is why the foundation layer can speak of Equilibrium
without qualification and have most readers understand it
rightly. The Equilibrium most things drift to is the
Equilibrium of the trivial frame.

## Rotated frames

A frame can be rotated, so that some other point in εσδ-space
becomes the thing's local Equilibrium. Two contributions
produce rotations:

- A **nature-frame** belongs to a thing for what kind of
  thing it is. A being whose nature aligns it with the
  [[source|Source]] carries a frame in which Source is
  Equilibrium. From its own perspective it is at rest, at
  home, at the natural centre; from an ordinary mortal's
  perspective it sits at the Source.
- A **place-frame** belongs to a region of space. Near a
  black hole, the geometry of the local region maps to
  εσδ-space such that [[chaos|Chaos]] is the local
  Equilibrium. Anything entering the region inherits that
  rotation while it remains there. The thing is not being
  pulled toward Chaos by some new force; it is drifting
  toward Equilibrium in the local frame, as everything
  always does — but local Equilibrium now points to Chaos
  in εσδ-coordinates.

A thing's effective frame is the composition of its
nature-frame with the place-frame it currently occupies. How
the two compose is left open at the foundation layer.

## What frames are not

A frame is not a tilt of the basin. The basin of Mundus is
not tilted; the map into it is rotated. The εσδ-geometry has
not changed; what has changed is which εσδ-point a particular
thing experiences as its centre.

A frame is not a force. Nothing pushes a thing _toward_ its
local Equilibrium any harder than the ordinary gravity of the
basin always pulls things to their local low point. Things
near a black hole drift Chaos-ward not because a new
attraction has arisen but because their local Equilibrium has
moved.

A frame is not a path. It says where a thing's local
Equilibrium is, not how the thing gets there. Drifting is
[[foundation/fabric/drifting|the motion]] along the gradient toward
local Equilibrium. Active motion against the gradient is
[[foundation/fabric/nearing|Nearing]].

## Realms and frames

Where clusters identify particular regions of Mundus as
realms — Aurethia knows [[realms/nareth|Nareth]] near the
Source, [[realms/valdor|Valdor]] near the Self,
[[realms/the-void|the Void Between Worlds]] near
Chaos — what they have named are regions of Mundus whose
place-frames are rotated toward a particular Cardinal. The
foundation layer registers that such regions exist; it does
not enumerate them or claim that every Cardinal-rotated
region is a realm of any cluster.

Whether some frames are rotated toward Horizons rather than
toward Cardinals is left open at the foundation layer. The
geometry does not forbid it.
