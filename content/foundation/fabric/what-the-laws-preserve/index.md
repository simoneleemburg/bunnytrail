The three foundation laws describe what diverges across
displacement. [[mundus-frame|Mundus Frame]] sets out how a
thing's local Equilibrium can sit anywhere in εσδ-space.
[[subjective-time|Subjective Time]] sets out how subjective
duration decouples from any shared clock as displacement grows.
[[continuity-of-passage|Continuity of Passage]] sets out how
the metric, and with it the answer to "which path is shortest,"
varies frame to frame.

What the three laws leave standing is what does **not** diverge.
Inside any frame the order of moments holds. Across all frames
the traveller pays. No displacement is gratis. The cosmology is
permissive in many directions and strict in a few — and the
strict commitments are worth naming, because most of what could
go strange about a world built on rotated frames and asymmetric
durations does not, in fact, go strange. This entry collects
those commitments.

A note before the commitments: each of them holds **generically**
— everywhere εσδ-space behaves the way the three laws describe.
εσδ-space is not guaranteed to behave that way everywhere. The
geometry permits non-generic structures — singularities,
points where the metric degenerates, connections that the
ambient geometry does not predict — at which the generic
behaviour locally fails. None of those exceptions reach the
laws themselves. They are exceptions to the **genericity** of
εσδ-space, not to its constitution. The closing section names
what room the geometry leaves for them.

## Local causality

Inside any single frame — Equilibrium or displaced — the order
of moments is preserved. A → B → C still hold. Memory still
works. Causes still precede their effects locally. Nothing in
the three laws is a licence to break the ordering of one's own
experience.

This is already explicit in Subjective Time ("from inside a
displaced existence, time does not feel strange") and implicit
in Continuity of Passage (the body's path is continuous in
ordinary space, so the events along it are encountered in some
order). Named here, it is the first structural commitment: a
frame is internally consistent. Whatever else displacement does,
it does not break the order of moments inside the frame doing
the displacing.

## No revisiting, generically

Generically, no frame can revisit its own past. The asymmetry
of Subjective Time runs one way only — subjective duration
accumulates against displacement, it does not unwind. There is
no symmetric direction in which a thing could move "backward"
in its own time. The basin is the floor. A traveller can sit at
Equilibrium or climb the slope; they cannot dig below the floor.

A consequence: throughout the generic geometry, there are no
closed loops in any frame's history. A traveller cannot, by any
ordinary practice or displacement, arrange to arrive at a moment
they have already lived. The frames may disagree about rates;
none of them folds back on itself.

What the geometry permits at non-generic points — singularities,
exotic connections — is left to the closing section. The
generic statement is what holds across the cosmology as a whole.

## No reaching across, generically

Generically, the fact that two frames' clocks disagree does not
let one frame reach into the other. A displaced traveller's
longer subjective duration is theirs; it does not give them a
vantage from which to act on the Equilibrium frame at moments
the Equilibrium frame has not yet reached.

The traveller's only way, generically, to deposit anything in
the Equilibrium frame is to return to it — and what they bring
is what their own subjective duration has carried through the
displacement and back. The two frames remain real to each other
only at the points where a continuous path connects them. Where
a non-generic structure provides such a connection that the
ambient geometry does not, the connection is still continuous;
what changes is which points it connects.

## No skipping the passage

By [[continuity-of-passage|continuity of passage]], no path
skips its passage. There is no teleportation. There is no
folded distance. The medium between any two ordinary-space
locations is what it is, and the body must cross it.

What displacement changes is the **rate** of crossing and the
**character** of the medium being crossed, not its extent.
A Knight piloting an [[kinds/eidolon|Eidolon]] across cluster
distances still traverses every metre. A traveller leaving a
[[mundus-frame|rotated region]] still walks the path that leads
out, however shaped by the lean. Nothing in Alteria arrives
without travel.

This commitment is the one that survives non-genericity intact.
A singularity or exotic connection does not let a body skip its
passage; it provides an unusual continuous path through itself.
The path is still walked. The other commitments above — on
revisiting and reaching across — admit local failures at
non-generic points. This one does not.

## The traveller pays

This is the deepest of the commitments and the one that closes
off the rest. Whatever a displacement offers, it is not free.
The lean is held by the leaner. The Nearing is sustained by
the practitioner. The Eidolon's crossing is held by the Knight
across the whole length of the journey, paid in their own
coherence. Subjective duration accumulates with displacement
because **the work of being displaced is what accumulates as
duration**. There is no configuration of frames in which a
traveller does more than the Equilibrium-frame measurement
suggests and does it for free; the extra is paid for in their
own subjective interval, their own metabolism, their own
coherence, their own depletion.

A traveller deep in a Cardinal-rotated region experiencing
months while only days pass at the centre is not exploiting a
loophole; they are **living those months**. Their body ages
through them. Their thinking moves through them. The
divergence is real on both sides because the work is real on
both sides — done by the traveller, against displacement, in
the traveller's own measure.

This rules out a whole category of phenomenon the cosmology
might otherwise permit. There is no sorting-by-rotation that
yields free configurations of ordinary material. There is no
nearing that reaches what an unleaned traveller could not reach
**for free** — what the lean reaches, it reaches by being the
lean, and the lean is paid for. There is no metric-shopping
that lets a body cross a long path as a short one without
doing the long-path work somewhere.

The geometry is permissive. The economy is strict.

Non-generic structure does not loosen the economy. A singularity
is not free passage; encountering one costs the traveller
something, often a great deal. An exotic connection is a feature
of the geometry and a thing of the geometry's own scale of cost.
The category the law rules out is _frame-asymmetry as a free
lunch_. What it does not rule out is local geometry that is
unusual and that asks its own price.

## What this is not

These commitments are not separate machinery layered on top of
the three laws. They are what the three laws **already say**,
read together for what they do not permit. Naming them is
editorial; the constraint is geometric.

They are also not a guarantee that displaced existence is safe
or symmetric or fair. A displacement can do enormous violence
to a traveller. What it cannot do is cheat — cannot deliver
something for nothing, cannot loop a frame back on itself,
cannot move a body without moving it through the space between.

The cosmology is structured this way by the shape of εσδ-space
itself: an asymptotic basin with no symmetric direction, a
unified Mundus that all frames sit within, a metric that
depends on the leaner but a topology that does not. What is
preserved is what the geometry preserves. The three laws are
where that preservation shows.

## Where the geometry is non-generic

The commitments above hold throughout the generic geometry —
which is to say, throughout the geometry as the three laws
describe it. εσδ-space is not bound to be generic everywhere.
The geometry permits, at isolated points or along isolated
structures, behaviour the laws' generic statements do not
predict.

Singularities are the clearest case. A point at which the
εσδ-curvature passes any finite bound, or at which the metric
degenerates, is a point at which "what is the shortest path
through here," "how does subjective duration accumulate
through here," and "what does it mean to continuously cross
here" all stop having the generic answers. The point is still
a point in Mundus; the laws governing the rest of Mundus still
describe the rest. The singularity is a local exception,
structurally, and is not described by the laws because the
laws describe genericity.

A black hole is the canonical material example. The mass of
the body curves εσδ-space around it — the same kind of
curvature that produces the [[mundus-frame|place-frame]]
rotation in its vicinity — and at the centre the curvature
becomes singular. Far enough out, the εσδ-geometry is curved
but generic, and the [[mundus-frame|Mundus Frame]] account
holds: travellers there sit in a Chaos-rotated place-frame and
drift Chaos-ward by the ordinary mechanism. Close to the
singularity itself, the generic account stops applying.
Anything that reaches such a point is no longer in the regime
the laws describe.

Exotic connections are the other case. The geometry of Mundus
does not forbid two ordinary-space locations being connected
by a path the ambient geometry does not predict — a connection
in the shape of a non-generic structure, contributed by the
geometry locally rather than the laws globally. Where such a
connection exists, the [[continuity-of-passage|continuity of
passage]] still holds: the connection is a continuous path,
and a body using it still traverses every point along it. What
the connection may do is link points that the ambient metric
would call far apart. It does not skip the passage; it
provides an unusual one.

The closed-room thought experiment is the canonical illustration.
A traveller leaning hard toward a Cardinal in a sealed room
expects, by the generic laws, to remain in the room. The walls
do not yield to the lean. The lean changes their subjective
duration, not the room's topology. But if the room contains
a singularity — small enough that an Equilibrium-frame
observer never registered it as a feature, large enough in
its εσδ-curvature that the leaning traveller's path is bent
into contact with it — the traveller may find themselves in
non-generic geometry, and what happens then is the
singularity's question to answer, not the laws'. They might
be destroyed. They might emerge somewhere else. They might be
changed in ways the generic geometry has no vocabulary for.
None of those outcomes violates the laws. The laws describe
what happens **away from** such points; they do not undertake
to describe what happens at them.

These exceptions are rare, structurally local, and do not
soften the generic commitments. A traveller does not "find" a
singularity by leaning; leaning is the same practice the
generic laws describe, and the generic outcome is the
overwhelmingly likely one. The carve-out exists because the
geometry permits it, not because the practice reaches it. Most
nearings, in most rooms, end exactly where the generic laws
predict.
