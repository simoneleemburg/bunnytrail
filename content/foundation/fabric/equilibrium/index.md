Equilibrium is the centroid of [[mundus|Mundus]] — the
position at which the three [[kinds/axis|axes]] of the
interior are equal, ε = σ = δ. The three
[[kinds/cardinal|Cardinals]] are equidistant from it; the
three [[kinds/horizon|Horizons]] are equidistant from it.
No bound pulls on a thing at Equilibrium any harder than
any other.

It is the opposite of a [[kinds/bound|bound]]. Where the
bounds are limits that things approach but cannot reach,
Equilibrium is an attractor: a soft basin in the geometry
into which things drift when nothing else acts on them.
Most massive things, in most places, spend most of their
time somewhere near the εσδ-centroid — because for most
things in most places, the centroid is the low point of
their basin.

Nothing rests exactly at Equilibrium. Real things wobble.
The point ε = σ = δ is precise; the region around it where
ordinary existence is found is soft-edged and lived-in. The
foundation layer treats Equilibrium as a region rather than
a point, in the same way Mundus itself is treated as an
interior rather than a set of coordinates: the geometry is
there to be lived inside, not stood on.

## Equilibrium and frame

The εσδ-centroid is the Equilibrium _of the trivial frame_ —
the frame in which a thing's nature and its place add no
rotation to the map between local experience and global
εσδ-geometry. For things with a non-trivial
[[mundus-frame|Mundus Frame]], the local Equilibrium sits
somewhere else in εσδ-space: a [[kinds/naya|Naya's]] local
Equilibrium is at the [[source|Source]]; a mortal's local
Equilibrium near a black hole drifts toward [[chaos|Chaos]].

The geometry has not changed. The basin still has a low point
and things still drift to it. What has changed is the map: a
different point in εσδ-space is, for this thing here, the
low point its drift heads toward. The foundation layer takes
"Equilibrium" without qualification to mean the εσδ-centroid;
"local Equilibrium" names whatever point a particular frame
picks out.

## Asymmetry with the bounds

The cosmology has six bounds and one Equilibrium. The
bounds repel — a thing approaches a Cardinal or a Horizon
only by [[foundation/fabric/nearing|nearing]] hard against the natural
give of the geometry, and the closer it gets the more
strongly it is pulled back toward the interior. Equilibrium
attracts — every thing drifts to its local Equilibrium when
nothing else is acting on it; only effort or some external
push displaces it.

Approaching a bound is something a thing _does_; approaching
Equilibrium is what a thing does when nothing else is
happening to it. This asymmetry is what makes the εσδ-centroid
the centre of ordinary existence: it is where everything with
a trivial frame ends up, and most things have trivial frames.

## Cluster accounts

How clusters name and theorise the Equilibrium varies. Some
treat it as a place; others as a state; others do not name
it at all and only describe the bounds. The foundation
layer commits to the geometric fact and leaves the rest.
