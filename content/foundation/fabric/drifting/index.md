Drifting is what a thing in [[mundus|Mundus]] does when
nothing else is acting on it. The basin of εσδ-geometry has
a low point at the [[equilibrium|Equilibrium]], and a thing
left to itself is pulled down the gradient toward that low
point. This is the same physics everywhere.

What differs from thing to thing, and from place to place,
is _where the local Equilibrium is in εσδ-space_. That is
fixed by the thing's [[mundus-frame|Mundus Frame]] — the
composition of its nature-frame and its place-frame. Drift
direction is the direction of the local Equilibrium in
εσδ-coordinates.

## The same motion, many directions

A mortal in an ordinary place has the trivial frame: local
Equilibrium is the εσδ-centroid, and drift is toward
ε = σ = δ. This is what most things do most of the time.

A mortal near a black hole has a rotated place-frame: the
black hole's mass curves the local εσδ-geometry, and the
displaced local Equilibrium sits toward the [[chaos|Chaos]]
cardinal. The same passive drift now pulls them Chaos-ward.
The mortal is not being attracted by a new force; the basin's
low point has moved in εσδ-coordinates because the local
εσδ-geometry is curved.

A [[kinds/naya|Naya]] anywhere has a rotated nature-frame:
local Equilibrium sits at the [[source|Source]], and the same
passive drift pulls them Source-ward wherever they are. From
their own perspective they are at rest at the centre; an
ordinary mortal's εσδ-frame describes them as sitting near
the Source.

In every case, the drift tendency is the same — the gradient
pulls toward local Equilibrium. The varieties of "where things
actually end up in εσδ-space" come entirely from variety in
the frames, not from variety in the physics.

## Tendency and trajectory

The basin and its gradients describe a **drift tendency** —
the directional pull that local εσδ-geometry exerts on a
thing at a given point in the space. Drift tendency is a
property of the field: it tells you which way is downhill.
It is not itself motion.

The realized path a thing takes through εσδ-space is its
**drift trajectory**. A trajectory is the product of drift
tendency, the thing's inertia, and any [[foundation/fabric/nearing|Nearing]]
interactions that have acted on it. Because trajectories
depend on history, a thing does not simply snap to local
Equilibrium the instant it stops being held. It arrives along
a path that reflects what it was doing before.

Inertia here is a property of the thing's identity: a
coherent, stable identity resists reorientation and carries
its trajectory forward even against a shifted local gradient.
A fragmented or recently ruptured identity offers little
resistance and falls quickly toward whatever basin the
geometry offers it.

This separation has several consequences:

- Two things with identical Mundus Frames, placed at the same
  εσδ-position, may follow different trajectories if they
  arrive with different inertial states.
- A trajectory can overshoot local Equilibrium and oscillate
  or orbit rather than descend directly to the low point.
- Frame lag is possible: a thing whose place-frame has changed
  — by entering or leaving a curved region — continues along
  its prior trajectory for a time before the new geometry
  asserts itself.
- Hovering is possible: sustained Nearing that exactly
  counteracts local drift tendency holds the trajectory in
  place without eliminating the underlying pull.

The contour lines and gradient diagrams for εσδ-space describe
drift tendency — where the basin slopes, how steep, which way
is down. They do not prescribe trajectories. The path any
given thing takes through that landscape is always also a
function of what it carries with it.

## Drifting and Nearing

Drifting is passive. The complement of drifting is
[[foundation/fabric/nearing|Nearing]] — an active orientation against
the gradient, toward some point in εσδ-space _other_ than the
thing's local Equilibrium. Nearing does not directly move a
thing; it applies a torque to the trajectory, altering the
orientation conditions that govern the path. A thing stops
drifting when something within it leans; the lean sustains
the reorientation for as long as it is held. Release it and
drift tendency resumes asserting itself against whatever
inertia the trajectory carries.

A thing cannot reach a [[kinds/bound|bound]] by drifting,
because the bounds are repellers — the gradient gets steeper
as a thing leans toward one, and no Equilibrium ever sits
exactly on a bound. Things near a black hole drift toward
Chaos but never reach it, because the εσδ-curvature moves
local Equilibrium _toward_ Chaos asymptotically without ever
placing it there. The asymmetry of the geometry holds in
every frame.

## Notation

The sigil for Drifting is **↧** — an arrow falling away from
the bar it rested on. It reads as motion taken by a thing
that has stopped holding itself, and lets the local gradient
carry it. (Compare
[[foundation/fabric/nearing|Nearing]], whose sigil **↥** is
the same shape inverted.)

The drift tendency always points toward local Equilibrium, so
the destination is fixed by the thing's [[mundus-frame|frame]]
and need not be written. Where the rotation of the place-frame matters and
clarity helps, the axis-sigil can be prepended to say which
way the local gradient is leaning the drift: **δ↧** for a
thing whose place-frame is rotated toward [[chaos|Chaos]] and
which is therefore drifting Chaos-ward, and analogously for
the other axes.
