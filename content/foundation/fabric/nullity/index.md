Nullity is the Horizon between [[self|the Self]] and
[[chaos|Chaos]] — the edge along which [[essence|Essence]] goes
to its minimum.

It is not a place; it is a limit. Along Nullity, subjective
reality and the work of unbinding are both present, in any
mixture, but there is no pattern in any of it — no kind being
instantiated, no form being borne, nothing the thing is _an
example of_. Experience without form; coming-apart without
anything coming-apart.

A thing in [[mundus|Mundus]] that leans hard toward Nullity
thins in its participation in pattern. It can still be intensely
the subject of its own world; it can still be unbinding. What
recedes is any sense of which kind it is, of which form it
shares with others, of the medium that lets it be _a thing of
that sort_. As with the [[kinds/cardinal|Cardinals]], the limit
is not reached. A thing with mass cannot have zero Essence any
more than it can have zero Subjectivity or zero Dissolution.

## The Essence axis

The axis whose far minimum is Nullity is named
**[[mundus#the-three-axes|Essence]]**: a thing's Essence is the
degree to which its character is its participation in a general
pattern rather than the particulars it happens to have. A thing
leaning hard along its Essence is leaning _away_ from Nullity
and toward [[source|the Source]].

## Relation to the other bounds

Nullity is one of three [[kinds/horizon|Horizons]] that run
along the edges of Mundus; [[oblivion|Oblivion]] and
[[tranquility|Tranquility]] are the other two. The opposite of
Nullity, in the sense the Essence axis defines, is not another
Horizon. It is the Cardinal [[source|the Source]] — the point
at which Essence goes to its maximum.

A thing positioned on Nullity is not at zero. It is leaning
along the edge — its Subjectivity and its Dissolution traded
against one another while its Essence recedes toward the limit
it cannot reach.
