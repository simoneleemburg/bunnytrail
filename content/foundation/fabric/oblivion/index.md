Oblivion is the Horizon between [[source|the Source]] and
[[chaos|Chaos]] — the edge along which
[[subjectivity|Subjectivity]] goes to its minimum.

It is not a place; it is a limit. Along Oblivion, pattern and
the work of unbinding are both present, in any mixture, but
there is no one in to whom any of it is happening. The form
persists or comes apart; nothing experiences either.

A thing in [[mundus|Mundus]] that leans hard toward Oblivion
thins on the inside. Its participation in pattern can stay
strong, its falling-apart can stay strong, but the perspective
from which any of that is anything-at-all recedes. As with the
[[kinds/cardinal|Cardinals]], the limit is not reached. A thing
with mass cannot have zero Subjectivity any more than it can
have zero Essence or zero Dissolution: some non-zero amount of
each is part of what it is to be a coherent thing-in-Mundus.

## The Subjectivity axis

The axis whose far minimum is Oblivion is named
**[[mundus#the-three-axes|Subjectivity]]**: a thing's
Subjectivity is the degree to which reality, for that thing, is
the experience of being it. A thing leaning hard along its
Subjectivity is leaning _away_ from Oblivion and toward
[[self|the Self]].

## Relation to the other bounds

Oblivion is one of three [[kinds/horizon|Horizons]] that run
along the edges of Mundus; [[nullity|Nullity]] and
[[tranquility|Tranquility]] are the other two. The opposite of
Oblivion, in the sense the Subjectivity axis defines, is not
another Horizon. It is the Cardinal [[self|the Self]] — the
point at which Subjectivity goes to its maximum.

A thing positioned on Oblivion is not at zero. It is leaning
along the edge — its Essence and its Dissolution traded against
one another while its Subjectivity recedes toward the limit it
cannot reach.
