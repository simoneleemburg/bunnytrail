Nearing is active, willed motion in [[mundus|Mundus]] against
the drift gradient — a thing leaning toward some point in
εσδ-space other than its local
[[equilibrium|Equilibrium]]. It is what a thing does that
[[foundation/fabric/drifting|drifting]] does not do.

The εσδ-geometry is the same everywhere; what makes nearing
hard is that the basin pulls every thing toward its own local
low point. A thing's [[mundus-frame|Mundus Frame]] sets that
low point. To near anywhere else is to lean against the
gradient that sets the thing's drift.

## Nearing a Cardinal

Any thing can in principle near any of the three
[[kinds/cardinal|Cardinals]] — the [[source|Source]], the
[[self|Self]], or [[chaos|Chaos]]. What differs from thing to
thing is _how hard each lean is_, because each thing nears
from a different starting position in εσδ-space.

A mortal at the εσδ-centre nears all three Cardinals with
about the same effort, and the effort increases as the lean
goes further. A [[kinds/naya|Naya]], whose nature-frame
already places its local Equilibrium at the Source, drifts
Source-ward without nearing at all; what would cost a mortal
sustained effort is, for a Naya, the absence of effort. The
Naya in turn nears the centre or the other Cardinals against
its own gradient.

This is the structural account of why some
[[kinds/tradition|traditions]] specialise in one direction of
nearing rather than another. The tradition's practitioners
near, but they near _from where their nature and place put
them_, and the direction that is hardest for one is easiest
for another.

## Nearing and reach

Nearing does not reach a Cardinal. The bounds are asymptotic;
the gradient steepens without limit as a thing leans further.
A long enough, hard enough nearing brings a thing closer to a
Cardinal than ordinary drift ever would, but the Cardinal
itself is never arrived at. The same is true of any nearing
toward a [[kinds/horizon|Horizon]] or any other point that
sits at a structural limit.

What nearing does reach is _some new position_ in εσδ-space
— a position the thing would not occupy if it were drifting.
Holding such a position takes ongoing leaning; letting the
lean go lets drift resume. Phenomena that look like sustained
displacement from ordinary existence — long-lived oracular
states, lives unusually free of decay, the kinds of transit
that move pilots between leaned-on regions — are at
foundation level sustained nearings, held against the
gradient by the practitioner.

## Cluster accounts

Where clusters describe particular practices of nearing —
Aurethian [[aurethia/fabric/nearing-source|Nearing toward the
Source]], or [[aurethia/fabric/remembrance|Remembrance]]
toward the Self, or other traditions — they are describing
structured ways their practitioners lean. The foundation
layer registers the structural fact: nearing is what it is to
lean against the local drift.
