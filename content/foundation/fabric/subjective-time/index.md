Time flows everywhere in [[mundus|Mundus]], but it does not run
at the same rate everywhere. Near
[[equilibrium|Equilibrium]] — the εσδ-centroid where the
trivial-frame basin has its low point — clocks run together.
Two things at rest at the centroid, drifting nowhere in
particular, accumulate the same duration. The shared moment-by-moment
ordering of ordinary life is the ordering of that frame.

Displacement from Equilibrium pulls a thing's subjective
duration away from the duration measured in the Equilibrium
frame. The further the displacement, the larger the divergence.
The asymmetry runs one way only: **subjective time runs longer
per unit of Equilibrium time as displacement grows**, never
shorter.

## What produces the displacement

There are two ways for a thing's experience to diverge from the
Equilibrium frame, and they compose:

- **A rotated [[mundus-frame|Mundus Frame]].** A thing whose
  local Equilibrium sits somewhere other than the centroid —
  whether because of its nature, the place it occupies, or both —
  is already displaced in εσδ-space. The displacement is fixed
  by the rotation; the divergence in subjective duration follows
  from it.
- **Sustained [[foundation/fabric/nearing|nearing]].** A thing
  leaning away from its local Equilibrium toward some other
  point in εσδ-space accumulates additional displacement for as
  long as the lean is held. The longer and harder the nearing,
  the further the displacement, the further subjective time
  diverges.

The two contributions add. A practitioner leaning hard toward a
[[kinds/cardinal|Cardinal]] from a rotated place-frame is
displaced both by the frame and by the lean.

## What the divergence looks like

From inside a displaced existence, time does not feel strange.
A → B → C still hold. Memory still works. Causes still precede
their effects locally. What changes is only the **rate** at
which subjective time accumulates relative to the shared frame.

From outside — from an observer at rest in Equilibrium watching
a displaced traveller — the traveller appears to inhabit more
time than is passing in the frame the observer keeps. A
traveller deep in a Cardinal-rotated region may live what feels
like months while only days pass for those who stayed at the
centre. A long, hard nearing of any Cardinal extends the
traveller's subjective duration the same way.

The divergence is real on both sides. The traveller does not
imagine the months; the observer does not imagine the days. The
two frames are simply not synchronised, and the displacement is
what desynchronises them.

## Why the divergence runs only one way

The asymptotic geometry of Mundus sets the direction. Equilibrium
is the basin's low point; the [[kinds/bound|Bounds]] —
Cardinals and [[kinds/horizon|Horizons]] alike — are
infinitely far away in the gradient. A thing displaced toward a
Bound is climbing a slope that steepens without limit. The
work of being at a displaced position — whether maintained by a
rotated frame or by an active lean — is what subjective time
accumulates against. A thing at Equilibrium does no such work
and accumulates no such excess.

There is no symmetric direction. A thing cannot be displaced
"deeper than Equilibrium" because Equilibrium is the basin's
floor. Subjective time can only run longer than Equilibrium time,
never shorter.

## The cross-cluster case

The clusters of Alteria sit at distances at which "when, exactly,
did that happen?" stops having a single answer.
[[space-time-and-identity-shift|Space-time and identity shift]]
handles the cross-cluster case in its own terms: there is no
universe-wide clock, and the only known way to bridge such
distances — sustained nearing — does not leave the traveller
unchanged. Subjective time is the foundation-level fact those
observations rest on. A nearing across cluster distances is a
sustained displacement; the traveller's subjective duration
diverges from any clock kept in either cluster's Equilibrium
frame.

## What this is not

Subjective time is not a metaphor for psychological time. A
distracted afternoon is not a displacement in εσδ-space. The
divergence is ontological: it depends on where the thing sits
in Mundus, not on how attentively it experiences its own
duration.

It is also not, generically, time travel. A displaced traveller
still moves forward in their own subjective time; an
Equilibrium-frame observer still moves forward in theirs.
Neither can, by displacement alone, revisit a prior moment of
their own. What the divergence permits is asymmetry between the
two rates, not reversibility. (Non-generic geometry — a
singularity, an exotic connection — is the carve-out set out in
[[what-the-laws-preserve|what the laws preserve]]; the law of
displacement does not undertake to describe what happens at
such points.)

And it is not action at a distance. The fact that two frames'
clocks disagree does not let either frame reach into the other.
What a displaced thing carries back into Equilibrium is whatever
it has, in its own subjective duration, brought through the
return.

It does not, finally, contract space. Displacement dilates
duration; it does not shorten the path. A displaced traveller
still crosses every metre of ordinary space their journey
includes, by the [[continuity-of-passage|continuity of passage]].
The two laws are complementary: time can run longer with
displacement, distance cannot run shorter. What the laws taken
together do **not** permit — revisiting, reaching across,
skipping the passage, getting something for nothing — is the
subject of [[what-the-laws-preserve|what the laws preserve]].
