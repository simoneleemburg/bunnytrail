Chaos is the layer of reality along which things come apart.

It is the axis of entropy, decay, dissolution — the dimension
along which what is coherent loosens, what is held releases,
what is bound unbinds. Not an opposing force pushing against
the other realms but a peer to them: one of the four ways
existence happens.

The other three realms account for things coming together,
staying together, and telling themselves they are together.
Chaos accounts for the rest of it — the side of reality in
which selves dissolve, bodies decay, narratives fragment, and
all of these things were always going to. A coherent thing
participates in Chaos throughout its existence, not only at
its end.

## Relationship to the other realms

Chaos is often spoken about as the opposite of the other
three, but the framing is misleading. The Source, the Self,
and Mundus describe how things hold themselves; Chaos
describes how things release. Both are constant; neither is
ever absent. A self in [[realms-of-existence/source|the
Source]] is always already loosening at its edges. A body in
[[realms-of-existence/mundus|Mundus]] is always already
decaying. A continuity in [[realms-of-existence/self|the
Self]] is always already forgetting parts of itself.

To call Chaos destructive is to mistake it for an event. It is
not an event. It is a condition of existing.

## Local accounts

No cluster, so far as the foundation layer registers, has
named Chaos in the same canonical way as the other three
realms. Aurethia's [[realms/the-void|Void Between Worlds]] is
in part a record of what falls through Chaos — broken Naya,
failed Eidolons, fragments of an older breaking — but the
order has not committed in public to a reading of the Void as
Aurethia's full account of the realm.

This is a gap, not a closed question.
