Essence is one of the three structural axes of
[[mundus|Mundus]]. It runs from
[[source|the Source]] — the Cardinal at which pure
pattern is unmixed with any particular instance — to
[[nullity|Nullity]], the Horizon along the Self–Chaos edge,
along which a thing participates in no pattern at all.

A thing's Essence is the degree to which its character is its
participation in a general pattern, rather than the particulars
it happens to have. Where
[[subjectivity|Subjectivity]] measures the give of
reality under the perceiver and
[[dissolution|Dissolution]] measures the failure of
the work that holds a thing together, Essence measures the
ratio of pattern to accident: how much of what a thing _is_ is
the kind it instantiates, and how much is the specific
arrangement of accidents that no kind requires.

## Walked toward the Source

Near the centre of Mundus, Essence is moderate. A particular
oak is recognisably an oak — the pattern is present, named,
operative — but the oak is also unmistakably _this_ oak, with
its specific scars, its lean, the bird's nest in the third
fork. The pattern and the particulars are mixed roughly evenly,
and the thing in question is at once a clear example of its
kind and a clear example of nothing but itself.

Leaning further toward the Source, the particulars begin to
thin. What is most present about a thing is the pattern it
instantiates; the specific accidents recede. A practitioner
leaning along this axis becomes less recognisably the specific
person they were and more recognisably the _kind_ of being they
are: their oracle-ness, their warrior-ness, their human-ness.
The signature mark fades; what remains is the form the
signature was a particular execution of.

Further still, even the bounded shape of the kind begins to
soften. The pattern is still present, but it is no longer
sharply distinguished from the patterns adjacent to it. The
oak shades toward tree-as-such; this particular grief shades
toward grief-as-such; the specific account a person held of
themselves shades toward selfhood-as-such. The categories that
let one kind be one kind and not another apply less and less.

At the Source itself — never reached — there is no instance
at all. There is only pattern, with nothing for the pattern to
be the pattern _of_.

## Walked toward Nullity

The other direction along the axis does not lead anywhere on
its own. Essence going down means a thing's participation in
pattern is becoming a smaller part of what it is — but _what_
is taking the pattern's place is set by the other two axes, not
by the loss of Essence itself. The position a thing arrives at,
as its Essence falls, is some point on [[nullity|Nullity]], the
Self–Chaos Horizon: the edge where Subjectivity and Dissolution
do all the leaning between them.

At the Subjectivity-leaning end of that edge — high
Subjectivity, low Dissolution, low Essence — a thing is densely
itself in a world that bends to its experience: a self so
particular and so attended-to that no general account reaches
it, in surroundings that answer to it alone. At the
Dissolution-leaning end — high Dissolution, low Subjectivity,
low Essence — a thing is densely itself in the way a thing
coming apart is densely itself: every fragment is irreducibly
its own fragment, with nothing it shares with any other.
Between these, along the Horizon, are the positions where the
trade-off between bending-reality and coming-apart is somewhere
in the middle.

This is what to make of the impressions ordinary thought
associates with low Essence — the artisan's signature mark, the
face that no description fits, the story that resembles no
other in outline. They are not products of low Essence alone.
They are products of being _somewhere on_ Nullity, with low
Essence as a precondition rather than a destination. The
signature mark of a beloved artisan, vividly held in the regard
of those who treasure it, leans more toward the Self than
toward Chaos; the signature mark of an abandoned, weathering
tool leans more toward Chaos than toward the Self. Both are low
in Essence; their differences come from the other two axes.

A thing's Essence on its own does not say which end of Nullity
it is closer to, only how far from the Source it sits.

## Not an independent dimension

Essence is not a quantity a thing has independently of its
Subjectivity or Dissolution. A thing in Mundus has one position
in the interior; Essence,
[[subjectivity|Subjectivity]], and
[[dissolution|Dissolution]] are three readings of
that one position. They sum to a constant. A thing whose
Essence is rising is, by the same fact, having its Subjectivity
or Dissolution (or both) lowered.

The geometric consequence is that as a thing thins toward
pattern, it moves across the interior, away from at least one
of the other two corners. A practitioner whose particulars are
thinning is, by that fact, no longer at exactly the position
they occupied before, and the position they have left is some
combination of give-under-the-subject and
work-of-holding-together.

## How clusters speak of it

Where a cluster's thought speaks of essence, of form, of the
real beneath the apparent, of the kind a thing belongs to, of
the wellspring from which selves emerge, of pure pattern or
ideal — it is often speaking of Essence or of some specific
manifestation of it. Where a cluster speaks of idiosyncrasy,
of signature, of the artisan's mark, of what is irreducibly
particular about a person or a place or a moment — it is often
speaking of positions on Nullity, of which low Essence is one
component.

In Aurethia, the controlled leaning toward the Source is named
[[fabric/nearing|Nearing]]: practitioners thin their specific
selves toward the resonant substrate, where what they share
with everything that has ever risen from that substrate becomes
more present than what is theirs alone. The region of Mundus
that leans hardest in this direction is known as
[[realms/nareth|Nareth]].

## A measure, not a verdict

Essence is not a measure of how _important_, _real_, or
_valuable_ a thing is. A thing high in Essence is not thereby
more genuine, and a thing low in Essence is not thereby
shallow. The axis names a ratio, not a rank. The artisan's
signature mark and the pure form the signature is an execution
of are both real, and neither is the truer version of the
other.

It is also not a measure of _generality_ in any cognitive
sense — not of how easy a thing is to describe, classify, or
fit into a category. A description's reach is a property of
the description; Essence is a property of the thing being
described. A thing thoroughly itself in ways no description
covers is low in Essence; the failure of description does not
make it high.

It is also not a measure of _recency_ or of _origin in time_. A
thing born yesterday is not automatically higher or lower in
Essence than a thing born a thousand years ago. "Closeness to
the pattern" is a structural relation, not a chronological one.

## What it does not mean

Essence is not a synonym for "spiritual," "sacred," or
"ideal." A thing leaning toward the Source is not thereby
transcendent; a thing leaning away is not thereby fallen. The
axis measures one specific kind of closeness — to the
Source-corner of Mundus, the Cardinal of pure pattern — and
nothing else.

It is also not a measure of _truth_. A thing high in Essence
is not thereby "what the thing really is." The particulars are
no less real for being particulars. Cluster traditions that
treat the patterns as the real and the particulars as the
illusory are reading their own philosophy into the axis; the
axis itself does not commit to that reading.

The word **essence** in ordinary use, including in this
compendium when uncapitalised, may mean any number of things:
the gist of a thing, an extract, a defining property. Only the
capitalised **Essence** names the structural axis.
