The clusters of Alteria are separated by distances at which the
question "when, exactly, did that happen?" stops having a single
answer. There is no universe-wide clock the records of one cluster
could be set against the records of another; the physics of
distance forbid it. An event in one cluster and an event in
another may be related causally, or not at all, but they are
never simply _simultaneous_.

Layered on top of this — and the older record, in Aurethia at
least — is [[aurethia/fabric/nearing|Nearing]], the only practice
that has ever been recorded to cross such distances. What Nearing
loosens is not only the practitioner's grip on linear time and
spatial distance but their grip on _who they are_: identity,
memory, emotion, bodily form. The three threads give way together,
and they give way because, at root, they are not as separate as
ordinary experience makes them feel. To cross a great distance by
Nearing is the same act as to drift loose of one's own moment
and the same act as to drift loose of one's own outline. The
practice does not pick and choose.

Whispers heard in [[aurethia/places/realms/nareth|Nareth]] bear
this out from the other side. They do not behave as if the past
has finished being there; some of them are reported to come from
ahead rather than from behind; and the voices in them are not
always cleanly the voices of one being. A practitioner deep
enough into Nearing is, in a sense, no longer at any one point on
a timeline, no longer at any one point in space, and no longer
quite the single bounded person they were when they began —
tilted obliquely to all three at once.

Read together, the layers say the same thing several times over.
There is no shared _when_ relating one cluster to another —
neither in the cold accounting of distance and signal, nor in
the warmer accounting of an Eidolon Knight's traversal — and the
warmer accounting comes with the further caveat that the traveller
themselves is, by the time they arrive, not precisely the
traveller who set out. Cross-cluster claims about timing — "this
happened during their such-and-such age," "that arrived before
our recorded history began" — are claims about what an
[[kinds/account|account]] records, not about an objective shared
moment the universe itself acknowledges. And cross-cluster claims
about a single continuous witness — "the same person was present
at both" — have to reckon with the fact that Nearing is the only
known way for a witness to be present at both, and Nearing does
not leave the witness untouched.

Aurethia's records of long memory — its four named ages — are an
Aurethia-frame label for Aurethian time. Other clusters, if they
ever acquire named ages, will name their own.
