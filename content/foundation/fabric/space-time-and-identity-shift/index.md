The clusters of Alteria are separated by distances at which the
question "when, exactly, did that happen?" stops having a single
answer. There is no universe-wide clock the records of one cluster
could be set against the records of another. Two events in two
clusters may be related causally, or not at all, but they are
never simply _simultaneous_.

This is the cross-cluster face of a more general fact. In
foundation terms, [[foundation/fabric/subjective-time|subjective
time]] only runs together near [[equilibrium|Equilibrium]];
displacement in εσδ-space pulls a thing's duration away from any
clock kept elsewhere. Cross-cluster distance is one such
displacement, sustained over a span large enough that ordinary
clocks have nothing to share. There is no shared _when_ relating
one cluster to another because there is no shared frame in which
to ask.

## The traveller is not the witness

Layered on top — and the older record, in Aurethia at least —
is [[aurethia/fabric/nearing|Nearing]], the only practice that
has ever been recorded to cross such distances. What Nearing
loosens is not only the practitioner's grip on linear time and
spatial distance but their grip on _who they are_: identity,
memory, emotion, bodily form. The three threads give way
together, and they give way because, at root, they are not as
separate as ordinary experience makes them feel. To cross a
great distance by Nearing is the same act as to drift loose of
one's own moment and the same act as to drift loose of one's
own outline. The practice does not pick and choose.

A practitioner deep enough into Nearing is, in a sense, no
longer at any one point on a timeline, no longer at any one
point in space, and no longer quite the single bounded person
they were when they began — tilted obliquely to all three at
once.

## What Nareth carries back

Whispers heard in [[aurethia/places/realms/nareth|Nareth]]
underline the same fact from the other side. They do not behave
as if the past has finished being there; some of them are
reported to come from ahead rather than from behind; and the
voices in them are not always cleanly the voices of one being.

These reports are not contact with the actual past or actual
future. Nareth is the [[source|Source]]-rotated region of
[[mundus|Mundus]]; what such a region contains is the local
material under the lean, not the [[kinds/cardinal|Cardinal]]
itself. The whispers are the Source-tilted texture that
self-and-time take on when they are pulled that way — the local
material of identity-and-duration expressing what becomes of it
under the lean. That texture honours no single timeline because
the lean does not, and the lean is what the practitioner is in
contact with.

## What cross-cluster claims are claims about

There is no shared _when_ relating one cluster to another —
neither in the cold accounting of distance and signal, nor in
the warmer accounting of an Eidolon Knight's traversal — and the
warmer accounting comes with the further caveat that the
traveller themselves is, by the time they arrive, not precisely
the traveller who set out.

Cross-cluster claims about timing — "this happened during their
such-and-such age," "that arrived before our recorded history
began" — are claims about what an
[[kinds/account|account]] records, not about an objective shared
moment the universe itself acknowledges. And cross-cluster claims
about a single continuous witness — "the same person was present
at both" — have to reckon with the fact that Nearing is the
only known way for a witness to be present at both, and Nearing
does not leave the witness untouched.

Aurethia's records of long memory — its four named ages — are an
Aurethia-frame label for Aurethian time. Other clusters, if they
ever acquire named ages, will name their own.
