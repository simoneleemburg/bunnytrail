Tranquility is the Horizon between [[source|the Source]] and
[[self|the Self]] — the edge along which
[[dissolution|Dissolution]] goes to its minimum.

It is not a place; it is a limit. Along Tranquility, pattern
and subjective reality are both present, in any mixture, but
nothing in the thing is coming apart. No work of unbinding;
nothing to lose hold of; nothing being undone. Form held in
experience, with the dissolution that ordinarily accompanies
holding-together absent.

A thing in [[mundus|Mundus]] that leans hard toward Tranquility
thins in its participation in the work of unbinding. It does
not stop changing — change can travel along the Essence and
Subjectivity axes — but the coming-apart character of change
recedes. As with the [[kinds/cardinal|Cardinals]], the limit is
not reached. A thing with mass cannot have zero Dissolution any
more than it can have zero Essence or zero Subjectivity: a
thing in Mundus is a thing whose holding-together is always at
some non-zero cost.

## The Dissolution axis

The axis whose far minimum is Tranquility is named
**[[mundus#the-three-axes|Dissolution]]**: a thing's
Dissolution is the degree to which the work of being one thing
is failing in it. A thing leaning hard along its Dissolution is
leaning _away_ from Tranquility and toward [[chaos|Chaos]].

## Relation to the other bounds

Tranquility is one of three [[kinds/horizon|Horizons]] that run
along the edges of Mundus; [[oblivion|Oblivion]] and
[[nullity|Nullity]] are the other two. The opposite of
Tranquility, in the sense the Dissolution axis defines, is not
another Horizon. It is the Cardinal [[chaos|Chaos]] — the point
at which Dissolution goes to its maximum.

A thing positioned on Tranquility is not at zero. It is leaning
along the edge — its Essence and its Subjectivity traded
against one another while its Dissolution recedes toward the
limit it cannot reach.
