The Source is the horizon of pure pattern.

It is not a place; it is a limit. Massive existence in
[[existence/mundus|Mundus]] can lean toward the Source — can
shade toward the kind it instantiates, toward the form
beneath the accidents, toward the participatory medium that
selves and things alike are drawn from — but cannot reach it.
The Source is asymptotic to mass.

A thing in Mundus that leans hard toward the Source thins in
its particulars. It does not become the Source. It is
something in Mundus that has approached one of Mundus's
limits.

## The Essence axis

The axis whose far limit is the Source is named
**[[existence/mundus#the-three-axes|Essence]]**: a thing's
Essence is the degree to which its character is its
participation in a general pattern rather than the particulars
it happens to have, and the higher a thing's Essence, the
closer it leans to the horizon.

A thing leaning hard along its Essence thins toward the form
it instantiates. The signature accidents recede; what becomes
foregrounded is the kind the thing is an example of. The
categories that let a thing be _this thing and not another of
its kind_ apply less and less as the horizon approaches; at
the horizon they do not apply at all.

At the Source itself, there is no instance. Nothing native to
the horizon would be _a particular_ in any sense those words
have inside Mundus.

## Relation to the other horizons

The Source bounds Mundus on one side; [[existence/self|the
Self]] and [[existence/chaos|Chaos]] bound it on others. The
three horizons are not opposed to one another, and they are
not opposed to Mundus. They are the geometry within which
Mundus takes its shape.

The distinction between the Source and the Self is
load-bearing and easily collapsed. The Source is the horizon
of pure pattern — of the form a thing instantiates, prior to
any particulars it might have. The Self is the horizon of
subjective reality — of a world that is wholly the experience
of the one undergoing it. A thing can lean toward both, but
the two leans are different leans.

## Local accounts

Aurethia knows the region of Mundus that leans hardest toward
the Source as [[realms/nareth|Nareth]] — the resonant
substrate, where identity, memory, and meaning are fluid and
the particulars of any one self are softened into what the
self shares with everything else that has risen from the same
ground. The [[kinds/naya|Naya]] are at home there. Aurethian
[[fabric/nearing|Nearing]] is a practice of controlled
approach: pilots lean toward the Source-horizon and return,
without becoming what they have leaned toward.

Whether anything is _at_ the Source — what something without
mass and native to the horizon would be — is not something the
foundation layer commits to. Aurethian thought has its own
readings; other clusters may differ.
