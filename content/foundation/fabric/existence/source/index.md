The Source is the horizon along which selves arise.

It is not a place; it is a limit. Massive existence in
[[existence/mundus|Mundus]] can lean toward the Source — can
shade toward resonance, toward identity-fluidity, toward the
participatory medium underneath any one self — but cannot reach
it. The Source is asymptotic to mass.

A thing in Mundus that leans hard toward the Source loosens at
the edges of itself. It does not become the Source. It is
something in Mundus that has approached one of Mundus's limits.

## The failure of time

The Source is the horizon at which temporal coherence ceases.
The axis whose far limit is the Source is named
**[[existence/mundus#the-three-axes|Resonance]]**: a thing's
Resonance is its closeness to its origin, the wellspring from
which selves emerge, and the higher a thing's Resonance, the
closer it leans to the horizon.

Inside Mundus, time is the dimension along which a thing
persists as itself: yesterday's body is today's body; what
remembers happening is what happened. As something leans toward
the Source — as its Resonance rises — that coherence loosens.
Identity-across-time becomes negotiable; resonance replaces
succession; the yesterday-and-today distinction softens into a
single participatory now.

At the Source itself, there is no time. The dimension along
which Mundus orders its events does not extend to the horizon.
Anything that did exist there — if anything does — would not
have a duration, would not have a before or an after, would not
be the same thing at one moment as at another in any sense
those words have inside Mundus.

This is the temporal face of the asymptote-to-mass: mass cannot
reach the Source because the Source is, among other things, the
place where time stops being defined, and mass is what persists
in time.

## Relation to the other horizons

The Source bounds Mundus on one side; [[existence/self|the
Self]] and [[existence/chaos|Chaos]] bound it on others. The
three horizons are not opposed to one another, and they are not
opposed to Mundus. They are the geometry within which Mundus
takes its shape.

The distinction between the Source and the Self is load-bearing
and easily collapsed. The Source is the horizon of emergence —
of selfhood _arising_, prior to any particular self being one.
The Self is the horizon of narrative continuity — of a self
_remembering having been one_. A thing can lean toward both,
but the two leans are different leans.

## Local accounts

Aurethia knows the region of Mundus that leans hardest toward
the Source as [[realms/nareth|Nareth]] — the resonant
substrate, where identity, memory, and meaning are fluid. The
[[kinds/naya|Naya]] are at home there. Aurethian
[[fabric/nearing|Nearing]] is a practice of controlled
approach: pilots lean toward the Source-horizon and return,
without becoming what they have leaned toward.

Whether anything is _at_ the Source — what something without
mass and native to the horizon would be — is not something the
foundation layer commits to. Aurethian thought has its own
readings; other clusters may differ.
