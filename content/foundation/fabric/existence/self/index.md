The Self is the horizon along which a being narrates itself.

Where [[existence/source|the Source]] is the horizon of
emergence, the Self is the horizon of continuity — the limit
along which a thing holds and tells a continuous account of
what it is. Pure remembering, pure ego, pure subjective
architecture without substrate to stand on.

Massive existence in [[existence/mundus|Mundus]] can lean
toward the Self — can shade toward narrative-as-geography,
toward eras-as-landscape, toward identity as something
externalised and walked through — but cannot reach it. The Self
is asymptotic to mass.

A being that leans hard toward the Self becomes more deeply
narrative, more deeply mnemonic. It does not become the Self.
It is a thing in Mundus that has approached one of Mundus's
limits.

## Relation to the other horizons

The Self is not the same as identity at [[existence/source|the
Source]]. The Source is where selves _arise_; the Self is where
they _remember having arisen_. The distinction is load-bearing.
Practices that approach the Source-horizon are not the same as
practices that approach the Self-horizon, even when they look
similar from outside.

Not only people lean along the Self. Places carry their own
continuity; objects carry their histories; civilisations carry
collective memory. Anything that persists across time leans
along the Self by however much its nature permits.

## Local accounts

Aurethia knows the region of Mundus that leans hardest toward
the Self as [[realms/valdor|Valdor]] — a liminal region where
memory becomes geography and inner architecture becomes
landscape. Aurethian [[fabric/valdor-traversal|Valdor
traversal]] is a practice of controlled approach: travellers
move deeper along the Self-axis and return, without becoming
what they have leaned toward.

Whether anything is _at_ the Self — what something without mass
and native to the horizon would be — is not something the
foundation layer commits to.
