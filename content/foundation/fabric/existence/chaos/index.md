Chaos is the horizon along which coherent things come apart.

Massive existence in [[existence/mundus|Mundus]] can lean
toward Chaos — can shade toward entropy, toward decay, toward
unbinding — but cannot reach it. Chaos is asymptotic to mass.
What in Mundus approaches Chaos hardest is what is breaking,
what is decaying, what is dissolving — but no broken thing has
become Chaos. It is a thing in Mundus that has leaned hard
toward one of Mundus's limits.

## Not an antagonist

Chaos is often mistaken for an opposing force pushing against
the rest of existence. The framing is misleading. The Source,
the Self, and Chaos are all horizons, all asymptotes, all the
same kind of limit. None of them is opposed to
[[existence/mundus|Mundus]] or to one another. Together they
are the shape Mundus has.

To call Chaos destructive is to mistake it for an event. It is
not an event. It is a direction, and one that everything in
Mundus leans toward by however much its nature permits, always.

## Local accounts

Aurethia knows the region of Mundus that leans hardest toward
Chaos as [[realms/the-void|the Void Between Worlds]] — the
medium between worlds, full of broken [[kinds/naya|Naya]],
fragments of an older breaking, and Eidolons that did not hold.
These are not at Chaos. They are massive things deep along the
Chaos-axis, broken-but-not-gone, leaning hard.

Whether anything is _at_ Chaos — what a thing native to the
horizon, without mass, would be — is not something the
foundation layer commits to.
