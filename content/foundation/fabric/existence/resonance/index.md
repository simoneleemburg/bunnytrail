Resonance is one of the three structural axes of
[[existence/mundus|Mundus]]. It is the measure of a thing's
closeness to [[existence/source|the Source]] — the corner of
Mundus at which selves arise and at which temporal coherence
ceases.

A thing's Resonance is the degree to which it is still close to
its origin: the degree to which it remains in contact with the
wellspring from which it, and every other self, first emerged.
Low Resonance is the ordinary condition of things that have
travelled some distance from where they came from, settled into
themselves, become the particular thing they are now and not
the broader thing they were drawn from. High Resonance is the
condition of things that are still near the wellspring — that
have not yet fully separated from it, or that have moved back
toward it by emergence, by practice, or by failure.

The image is something like a note still ringing with the
string it was struck from. Low Resonance is the note continuing
on its own; high Resonance is the note still co-vibrating with
the string. Both are the note; the difference is how much of
the source is still present in it.

## Not an independent dimension

Resonance is not a quantity a thing has independently of its
Recollection or Dissolution. A thing in Mundus has one position
in the interior; Resonance, [[existence/recollection|
Recollection]], and [[existence/dissolution|Dissolution]] are
three readings of that one position. The three sum to a
constant. A thing whose Resonance is rising is, by the same
fact, having its Recollection or Dissolution (or both) lowered.
The geometry will not permit otherwise.

## How clusters speak of it

Many of the words clusters reach for when describing closeness
to origin — resonance, attunement, the participatory now, the
divine, the source-of-things, the ground of being — are local
vocabularies for Resonance or for some specific manifestation
of it. Where a tradition speaks of returning to a wellspring,
of communion with what one came from, of dissolving the
individuated self into a prior shared layer, it is often
speaking of motion along this axis. The foundation-level name
is reserved for the structural quantity; it does not endorse
any one cluster's account, and in particular it does not commit
to any cluster's reading of the Source as divine, sacred, or
intentional.

The Aurethian practice of [[fabric/nearing|Nearing]] is a
controlled raising of Resonance, undertaken deliberately and
returned from.

## What it does not mean

Resonance is not a synonym for "spiritual," for "mystical," or
for "less embodied" in any general sense. A thing with low
Resonance is not thereby "shallow" or "ordinary"; a thing with
high Resonance is not thereby "transcendent." The axis measures
one specific kind of closeness — to the Source-corner of
Mundus, with its specific failure-mode (temporal coherence
loosening) — and not anything else.

It is also not a measure of _recency_. A thing born yesterday
is not automatically higher in Resonance than a thing born a
thousand years ago; "still close to one's origin" is a
structural relation, not a chronological one. What makes
Resonance high is participation in the wellspring, however that
participation is held — by being newly emergent from it, by
being in deliberate contact with it, by being unable to fully
separate from it.

The word **resonance** in ordinary use, including in this
compendium when uncapitalised, may mean any number of things:
sympathetic vibration, emotional weight, mnemonic echo. Only
the capitalised **Resonance** names the structural axis.
