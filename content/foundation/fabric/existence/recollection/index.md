Recollection is one of the three structural axes of
[[existence/mundus|Mundus]]. It is the measure of a thing's
closeness to [[existence/self|the Self]] — the corner of Mundus
at which narrative continuity is total and at which spatial
coherence ceases.

A thing's Recollection is the degree to which it is constituted
by the continuous narrative it carries — the degree to which
what it is, is what it remembers having been. Low Recollection
is the condition of things whose present existence is barely
informed by what they were before: a stone weathering, an
infant before memory, anything that is simply what it is in
this moment. High Recollection is the condition of things in
which the past presses hard against the present — beings deep
in memory, places saturated with the events that happened in
them, civilisations whose every decision is shaped by the long
story they tell about themselves.

## Not an independent dimension

Recollection is not a quantity a thing has independently of its
Resonance or Dissolution. A thing in Mundus has one position in
the interior; [[existence/resonance|Resonance]], Recollection,
and [[existence/dissolution|Dissolution]] are three readings of
that one position. They sum to a constant. A thing whose
Recollection is rising is, by the same fact, having its
Resonance or Dissolution (or both) lowered.

## How clusters speak of it

Where a cluster's thought speaks of memory, of narrative
identity, of the persistence of self through time, of
inner-architecture-as-landscape, of remembered-having-been —
it is often speaking of Recollection or of some specific
manifestation of it. Earth-philosophy's accounts of personal
identity over time, Aurethian thought about ego and the
remembered self: both are local vocabularies for the same
underlying axis.

The Aurethian practice of [[fabric/valdor-traversal|Valdor
traversal]] is a controlled raising of Recollection,
undertaken deliberately and returned from.

## What it does not mean

Recollection does not measure the _accuracy_ of memory, nor the
_amount_ of remembered material, nor the quality of any
particular act of remembering. A being can be high in
Recollection while forgetting most of its specific past, if
what it is is shaped pervasively by what it has been; a being
can be low in Recollection while having flawless recall of
particular events, if those events do not constitute its
present self.

The word **recollection** in ordinary use, including in this
compendium when uncapitalised, means the ordinary act of
remembering. Only the capitalised **Recollection** names the
structural axis.
