Recollection is one of the three structural axes of
[[existence/mundus|Mundus]]. It is the measure of a thing's
closeness to [[existence/self|the Self]] — the corner of Mundus
at which narrative continuity is total and at which spatial
coherence ceases.

A thing's Recollection is the degree to which what it is, is
what it has made of itself out of what it has been. Where
[[existence/resonance|Resonance]] measures closeness to a thing's
origin, Recollection measures closeness to a thing's _gathered
self_ — the figure assembled by the long work of carrying its
own history forward, choosing what of it to keep, and becoming a
particular continuous someone in consequence. Low Recollection
is the condition of things that have done little of this work,
either because they are too young for it, or too brief, or
because their nature does not include the work of self-making
at all: a stone weathering, an infant before memory, a wave
breaking. High Recollection is the condition of things in which
this work has gone far — beings who are deeply who they have
made themselves to be, places whose character is the long
accumulation of what has happened there, civilisations whose
present shape is the deliberate inheritance of their history.

The shift from low Recollection to high is not the accumulation
of memory; it is the constitution of identity _through_ the
holding of one's own history. A being can have remembered
everything that happened to it and remain low in Recollection,
if those events have not been gathered into a self. A being can
have forgotten most of its specifics and be high in
Recollection, if what it is is densely shaped by what it has
been.

## Not an independent dimension

Recollection is not a quantity a thing has independently of its
Resonance or Dissolution. A thing in Mundus has one position in
the interior; [[existence/resonance|Resonance]], Recollection,
and [[existence/dissolution|Dissolution]] are three readings of
that one position. They sum to a constant. A thing whose
Recollection is rising is, by the same fact, having its
Resonance or Dissolution (or both) lowered.

The geometric consequence is that becoming more fully oneself
moves a thing across the interior, away from at least one of
the other two corners. Resonance and Recollection are not
opposites in any moral or developmental sense — both are real
positions in Mundus — but rising in one means the geometry has
to give somewhere else: in Resonance, in Dissolution, or in
some mix of the two. A thing deeply gathered into who it has
made itself to be is, by that fact, no longer at exactly the
position it occupied before, and the position it has left is
some combination of closeness-to-origin and
closeness-to-coming-apart.

## How clusters speak of it

Where a cluster's thought speaks of memory, of narrative
identity, of the persistence of self through time, of
inner-architecture-as-landscape, of self-actualization, of
becoming-who-one-is — it is often speaking of Recollection or
of some specific manifestation of it. Earth-philosophy's
accounts of personal identity over time, Aurethian thought
about ego and the remembered self: both are local vocabularies
for motion along this axis.

The Aurethian practice of [[fabric/valdor-traversal|Valdor
traversal]] is a controlled raising of Recollection,
undertaken deliberately and returned from.

## What it does not mean

Recollection does not measure the _accuracy_ of memory, nor the
_amount_ of remembered material, nor the quality of any
particular act of remembering. The work it measures is the work
of being constituted by one's history, not the work of
recalling it.

It is also not a measure of _age_. An old thing is not
automatically higher in Recollection than a young one. Many
ancient things have done little of the work of self-making and
remain low in Recollection; some quite young things have done
much of it and are surprisingly high. What raises Recollection
is the gathering, not the duration.

The word **recollection** in ordinary use, including in this
compendium when uncapitalised, means the ordinary act of
remembering. Only the capitalised **Recollection** names the
structural axis.
