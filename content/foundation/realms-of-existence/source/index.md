The Source is the layer of reality from which selves arise.

It is not a place. Nothing is in the Source in the way that
things are in a room or on a planet. It is, instead, the
underlying medium of emergence — the dimension along which what
becomes a self becomes one. Identity, resonance, the inchoate
material out of which particular beings cohere: all of it
belongs to the Source.

A thing is not made _of_ the Source the way it is made of
matter. It draws _from_ the Source, the way a note draws from
the field of tones it could have been. The Source is what is
underneath the particular before the particular is chosen.

## Relationship to the other realms

The Source pairs with [[realms-of-existence/mundus|Mundus]] as
emergence pairs with embodiment: what becomes a self in the
Source persists as a body in Mundus. It pairs with
[[realms-of-existence/self|the Self]] as arising pairs with
narrating: what comes into being from the Source comes to
remember and tell itself in the Self. It stands across from
[[realms-of-existence/chaos|Chaos]] not as good against evil
but as cohesion against dissolution — the Source is where
selves come together; Chaos is the axis along which they come
apart.

## Local accounts

Aurethia knows the Source as [[realms/nareth|Nareth]] — the
resonant layer, where identity, memory, and meaning are fluid
and interconnected. Aurethian practice, particularly
[[fabric/nearing|Nearing]], works directly with the Source-side
of reality. The [[kinds/naya|Naya]] are native to it.

Other clusters may have their own accounts, or may not have
articulated one yet.
