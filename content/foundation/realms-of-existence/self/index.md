The Self is the layer of reality along which a being narrates
itself.

Where [[realms-of-existence/source|the Source]] is the axis of
emergence and [[realms-of-existence/mundus|Mundus]] is the axis
of embodiment, the Self is the axis of memory and ego — the
dimension along which an emerged, embodied thing holds and
tells a continuous account of what it is. To remember; to mean
what one says one means; to be the same person who did the
thing yesterday: all of it belongs to the Self.

Not only people participate in the Self. Places carry their
own continuity; objects carry their histories; civilisations
carry collective memory. Anything that persists across time
holds some thread of self-narrative, however thin. The Self is
the layer in which those threads are real.

## Relationship to the other realms

The Self is not the same as identity in
[[realms-of-existence/source|the Source]]. The Source is where
a self emerges as a self at all; the Self is where that self
remembers having been one. The distinction is load-bearing.
Practices that loosen identity at the Source level
(dissolution, deep resonance work) are not the same as
practices that traverse the Self layer (memory work, narrative
transformation), even when they look similar from outside.

The Self stands across from
[[realms-of-existence/chaos|Chaos]] as continuity stands
against unraveling — what the Self holds together, Chaos
loosens.

## Local accounts

Aurethia knows the Self as [[realms/valdor|Valdor]] — a
liminal realm of memory and subjective reality, navigable by
those who undergo
[[fabric/valdor-traversal|Valdor traversal]]. Aurethian framing
treats Valdor as somewhere a traveller can go; the foundation
layer treats it as a dimension every existing thing already
participates in. Both are true accounts; they differ in
vantage.

Other clusters may have their own accounts, or may not have
articulated one yet.
