Mundus is the layer of reality along which things are
embodied.

It is the material whole: matter, form, the persistent stuff
that occupies space and continues from one moment to the next.
Where [[realms-of-existence/source|the Source]] is the axis of
emergence and [[realms-of-existence/self|the Self]] is the axis
of narrative continuity, Mundus is the axis of physical
continuity — bodies, objects, geography, the cosmos as a
material arrangement.

Most of what is studied as physics, in any cluster, is the
study of Mundus from the inside. The orbits of worlds, the
chemistry of substances, the structure of bodies: all of it is
the Mundus-layer of reality being articulated by beings who
themselves participate in it.

## Relationship to the other realms

Mundus pairs with [[realms-of-existence/source|the Source]] as
embodiment pairs with emergence. A self that arises in the
Source comes to have a body in Mundus. Pairs with
[[realms-of-existence/self|the Self]] as the body pairs with
the story told about it.

Stands across from [[realms-of-existence/chaos|Chaos]] as
order against disorder, persistence against decay. A body
holds in Mundus until Chaos takes it apart.

## Local accounts

Aurethia knows Mundus as [[realms/asthera|Asthera]] — the
material, embodied layer, counterpart to
[[realms/nareth|Nareth]]. The Aurethian framing emphasises
Asthera as one pole of a paired cosmology with Nareth; the
foundation layer registers Mundus as one of four peer realms,
of which the Aurethian pair names two.

Other clusters may have their own accounts, or may not have
articulated one yet.
