The **Nguwari** [[bay]] are a rare amphibious people of Asthera —
serpentine in body, humanoid in bearing — native to
[[bayurinda|Bayurinda]]. Their Bayurindan name glosses as
_border swimmers_: the people who cross the half-tide line, the
seam between the archipelago's upper world and
[[nuunlau|Nuunlau]]. They are not of the deep, and they are not
of the surface alone; they are the kindred at the threshold
between the two.

The Nguwari share habitat with the archipelago's
[[kinds/human|human]] majority — present on the islands and
along the shores — but lean toward the tidal cave systems,
where the line between sea and stone is widest and a body
that moves freely between water and rock has the most room
to live. The compendium records them so far through this
threshold setting, and through a single member,
[[characters/ngonawan-ilyardi|Ngonawan Ilyardi]], the Sacred
Serpent of Resonance and Oracle of Interpretation in the
[[culture/oracle-triad|Oracle Triad]].

What general claims the compendium can make about the
Nguwari beyond this — their numbers, their distribution
across Bayurinda's archipelagos and cave-systems, the part
their bodies and languages play in the work some among them
are known for — wait on further entries. What is recorded
of them lives, for now, in the entries of the individuals
who belong to them.
