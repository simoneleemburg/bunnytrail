# Kael of the Third Veil

They say Kael was born in [[places/duskmere|Duskmere]] on a night when the Third
Veil thinned to gauze, and that for the first hour of his life the bells of the
border-watch rang of their own accord.

He does not deny it. He does not confirm it. He walks.

## The Road North

In his younger years he was seen most often on the salt road, a single staff
across his shoulder and a coat the colour of wet stone. Travellers who shared
his fire reported that he sometimes spoke of cities they had not yet visited as
though he had already left them behind.

## The Veil

The nature of the Third Veil is debated even among those who have crossed it.
What is agreed: it answers, sometimes, to those who name it carefully, and
Kael appears to know its name.
