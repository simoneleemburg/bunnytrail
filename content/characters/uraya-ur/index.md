Uraya Ur is the serving Oracle of Witnessing within the
[[social/oracle-triad|Oracle Triad]] of the
[[social/sisters-of-bellona|Sisters of Bellona]] — the Watcher of
Spiraling Echoes, in the order's older formal speech.

She is of the [[beings/naya|Naya]]: a primordial being native to
[[concepts/nareth|Nareth]], the resonant substrate. Like all Naya,
she has no fixed form there; identity and shape move with
resonance rather than holding to any one settlement. Her presence
in the Triad rests on a long, carefully tended binding with the
Sisters — the relationship that gives her enough purchase on
[[concepts/asthera|Asthera]] to take part in the order's work
without losing the Naretic vantage that is the whole point of her
role.

## In the Triad

As Oracle of Witnessing, Uraya perceives what cannot be perceived
from inside form. Astheran observers — even very disciplined ones —
read time as a sequence and meaning as a story; the line of one
thing then another. Witnessing reads it differently: as recursion,
spiral, the pattern that appears only across collapse and
re-emergence, the same shape returning in unlike circumstances.

Where the Oracle of Interpretation makes signal into structure and
the Oracle of Endurance carries structure through time, Uraya
watches the long shape that all of those passages trace out. Her
contributions to the Triad are rarely tidy. They are, almost by
definition, the things the other two would not have noticed: the
old echo in a new event, the pattern of forgetting that surrounds
a remembered thing, the recurrence beneath what looked like
novelty.

## Identity and form

Uraya is inherently gender-fluid and multiform — not as a choice
made in any one moment, but as the basic condition of being a
Naya. Her form in any encounter answers to resonance: to whom she
is speaking, what is at stake, what truth she is reaching for. The
Sisters use _she_ and _they_ interchangeably in their records, and
Uraya has expressed no preference between them.

That her own selfhood resists fixity is part of why she can do
what she does. Witnessing, in the Triad's understanding, is a mode
of perception that requires the perceiver not to be too settled.
The other Oracles bring discipline against drift; Uraya brings the
drift, held inside the discipline of the three.
