# QUESTIONS.md

Open threads — world-expanding questions worth returning to. Not a
roadmap, not a backlog. A running list of "this would be interesting
to figure out one day."

The rules:

- **Add freely.** If a question comes up while editing and isn't
  going to be answered now, drop it here in one line.
- **No status, no priority.** This is a list, not a tracker. If a
  question gets answered, strike it through or delete it — whichever
  feels right.
- **Questions, not assertions.** "What language do the urouthi
  speak among themselves?" is a question. "The urouthi probably
  speak a tonal language" is canon-by-implication and doesn't
  belong here.
- **Group when it's useful, not before.** Start flat. Once enough
  questions accumulate that scanning gets hard, split into sections
  (e.g. `## Places`, `## Beings`, `## Languages`).

## Open

### Languages & names

- What does _Bal Rochan_ mean literally in Deep Speech?
- What is the Old Tongue's relation, if any, to the spoken tongue of the drowned surface civilisation?
- What is the provenance of the word _Eidolon_ — Thallish, an order coinage, or something older?
- What is the provenance of the name _Sharazan_?
- What is Leyla's name in Tholingian, and what does it mean?
- What is Tholingian like — phonology, grammar, script? What are its naming customs?
- What other Nebelheimic tongues exist alongside Tholingian, and where are they spoken?
- What do the older Nuunlaumud call themselves in their own tongue (the surface only has the Bayurindan name)?
- What Deep-Speech names do the Urouthi use for the cocoon, the installation rite, and the Vochran-and-additive mixture?

### Peoples & kinds

- What is the Urouthi self-conception with respect to the ancestral inscriptions they steward — descendants, custodians, students, something else?
- What are the full axes of the internal Urouthi debate about further bodily change beyond the foundational modification?
- How did the older Nuunlaumud breathe in Nuunlau before Vochran existed — or did Vochran predate the Urouthi?
- What is the older Nuunlaumud's relationship to Naya, if any? Do they Near?
- Who are some named Nearborn in the historical record?

### Places & cosmology

- What caused the drowning? What do the deep call it in their own tongues?
- How many moons does Bayurinda have, and what are they called?
- What are Sharazan's named regions, cultures, and political shapes?
- How many World Pillars are there in Tholingia, and what counts as one?
- What are the charted regions of the Void? Which Knights have travelled it deliberately?

### Eidolons, Naya, traditions

- What is the origin story of the Oracle Triad?
- What happens to Uraya-Ur if the Pilgrim–Oracle partnership ends?
- Which Knight pilots the Eidolon paired with Viynangor Viyar?
- Whose "shattered god" is which, across the traditions that reference one?
- Does any Aurethian tradition deliberately practise nearing-Chaos, or is the cluster's silence on the question itself an artefact of curation — by the Knights, by the Triad, by something older? Is the absence honest or curated?

### Materials

- What is Vochran, structurally — a liquid, a colloid, a living medium, something else?
- Are the older ruin-structures on the Nuunlau seafloor an active source of Urouch, or only places where ancient Urouch was concentrated?

### Cosmology — frames

- How do a thing's nature-frame and the place-frame it occupies compose? Vectorially, multiplicatively, something stranger? Can a Naya near a black hole have its Source-rotation cancelled by the place's Chaos-rotation?
- Are there Mundus Frames rotated toward a Horizon rather than a Cardinal? If so, what realms or phenomena correspond to them?
- Is the εσδ-centroid privileged for any reason beyond being the trivial-frame Equilibrium, or is its centrality entirely an artefact of "most things have trivial frames"?
- Do frames change continuously across space, or are there frame-discontinuities (sharp transitions between rotation regimes)? What happens at one?
