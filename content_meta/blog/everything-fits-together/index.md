I still can’t believe this.

It’s day 4 now of working on this world-building compendium,
and my mind is blown by one realization.

Everything I’ve world-built over the last years, for as long as I’ve kept it somewhere.
This goes back at least 5 years of recorded — unrecorded? Much, much longer.
Spread across Google Docs, an old World Anvil account, my blog, even a paper notebook…
It all fits together.

But I never consciously understood this until now.
I finally recognized the shape it already had.
And this is mind-boggling.

This collective universe I’ve been unconsciously building?
It stretches not just across a few adjacent planets in the same star system. Not just across different planes of existence overlapping with the physical.
It stretches across time, deeply into mythology.
Across galaxies.

It includes Earth.
Yes, Earth.
With fictional history I’ve been weaving into it.

It includes the future — both the near future and the far, far future. It includes an Ice Age civilization making contact with the inhabitants of Aurethia across space and time.
And somehow, it is all deeply spiritual.

Let’s keep exploring.
Honestly, I can’t stop.
It keeps drawing me deeper. Further into the rabbit hole.
