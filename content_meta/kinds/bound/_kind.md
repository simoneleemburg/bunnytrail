A bound is not a place. It is a limit — a direction along
which things can lean, and a condition things can approach, but
a state massive existence cannot reach.

Six bounds enclose the interior of existence, in two shapes:

- The three **[[kinds/cardinal|Cardinals]]** are the corners of
  the interior — [[source|the Source]],
  [[self|the Self]], and [[chaos|Chaos]].
  Each is the limit of one of the three axes of
  [[mundus|Mundus]] going to its maximum.
- The three **[[kinds/horizon|Horizons]]** are the edges
  between the Cardinals — [[tranquility|Tranquility]] (between
  the Source and the Self), [[oblivion|Oblivion]] (between the
  Source and Chaos), and [[nullity|Nullity]] (between the Self
  and Chaos). Each is the locus on which one of the three axes
  goes to its minimum and the other two do all the leaning.

None of the six is opposed to Mundus or to one another. They
are the geometry within which Mundus is shaped.

## The mass condition

Approaching a bound is asymptotic to mass. The closer a thing
leans toward any bound, the more its mass resists — the same
shape as the relativistic limit on Earth, where reaching the
speed of light is asymptotic to mass and forbidden to anything
with rest mass.

A thing with mass cannot be at a bound. It can only approach
one, from inside Mundus, by however much its nature permits.
This is as true of the Horizons as of the Cardinals:
a thing with mass cannot have zero Essence, zero Subjectivity,
or zero Dissolution any more than it can have those quantities
maximised, because the conditions that let a thing be a coherent
thing-in-Mundus fail in both directions.

Whether anything _without_ mass is native to the bounds — and
what such a thing would be — is left open. The foundation layer
does not enumerate it; different clusters' accounts vary in
what they name and how.

## Local accounts

Particular clusters give the Cardinals their own names and
describe the regions of Mundus that lean closest to each.
Aurethia knows the region of Mundus closest to
[[source|the Source]] as
[[realms/nareth|Nareth]]; the region closest to
[[self|the Self]] as [[realms/valdor|Valdor]]; the
region that leans hardest toward [[chaos|Chaos]] as
[[realms/the-void|the Void Between Worlds]]. None of these are
the bounds themselves — they are the parts of Mundus nearest
to them.

No cluster has yet given the three Horizons names of its own,
though particular cluster phenomena may describe positions
along one without naming the Horizon as such.
