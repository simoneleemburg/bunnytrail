A Cardinal is a corner-bound of [[existence/mundus|Mundus]] —
the asymptotic limit of one of the three axes going to its
maximum. Three Cardinals sit at the corners of the interior of
existence: [[existence/source|the Source]] (the limit of
[[existence/essence|Essence]]), [[existence/self|the Self]]
(the limit of [[existence/subjectivity|Subjectivity]]), and
[[existence/chaos|Chaos]] (the limit of
[[existence/dissolution|Dissolution]]).

Each Cardinal is asymptotic to mass: a thing with mass can
lean toward a Cardinal — can shade toward pure pattern, toward
the world as its experience, toward unbinding — but cannot
reach it. At a Cardinal itself the categories that let a thing
be a thing-in-Mundus do not apply at all.

The Cardinals are one of the two shapes of
[[kinds/bound|Bound]] that bound Mundus. The other is the
[[kinds/horizon|Horizon]]: the edge between two Cardinals, the
locus on which the axis _opposite_ that edge goes to its
minimum. The two shapes are bounds in the same sense — both
asymptotic to mass — but a Cardinal is the limit of _one_ axis
going to its maximum, while a Horizon is the limit of one
axis going to its minimum.

## Local accounts

Particular clusters name the regions of Mundus that lean
closest to each Cardinal in their own vocabularies. Aurethia
knows the region closest to the Source as
[[realms/nareth|Nareth]]; the region closest to the Self as
[[realms/valdor|Valdor]]; the region that leans hardest toward
Chaos as [[realms/the-void|the Void Between Worlds]]. None of
these are the Cardinals themselves — they are the parts of
Mundus nearest to them.
