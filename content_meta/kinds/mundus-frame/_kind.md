A Mundus Frame is the map between a thing's local experience
of [[mundus|Mundus]] and the global εσδ geometry. The εσδ
geometry itself — the triangle, the three
[[kinds/cardinal|Cardinals]], the three
[[kinds/horizon|Horizons]], the [[equilibrium|Equilibrium]] at
the centroid — is fixed and the same everywhere. What varies
is _which point in εσδ-space_ counts as Equilibrium _for a
given thing in a given place_.

For most things in most places, the frame is the trivial one:
the local Equilibrium coincides with the εσδ-centroid, and
"downhill" in the thing's experience points toward ε = σ = δ.
For some things, or in some places, the map is rotated.
Things near a black hole experience the
[[chaos|Chaos]]-cardinal as their local Equilibrium; the
[[beings/naya|Naya]] experience the [[source|Source]] as
theirs. The basin of Mundus has not changed shape — the map
into it has rotated.

A thing's effective frame is composed of two contributions:
its **nature-frame**, intrinsic to what kind of thing it is,
and its **place-frame**, intrinsic to where in space it
currently is. Either may be the trivial map, in which case
the composition is whichever is not.

Frames are how the foundation layer accounts for the variety
of [[fabric/drifting|drift]] directions in Mundus without
multiplying basins. There is one geometry; there are many
frames into it.
