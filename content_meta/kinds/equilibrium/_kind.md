An Equilibrium is the centroid of [[mundus|Mundus]] — the
position at which the three [[kinds/axis|axes]] are equal,
none of the three [[kinds/cardinal|Cardinals]] is closer than
the others, and none of the three [[kinds/horizon|Horizons]]
is closer than the others.

Where a [[kinds/bound|bound]] is asymptotic — a limit a thing
can approach but cannot reach — the Equilibrium is the
opposite. It is not a limit but an attractor. Massive things
in Mundus drift toward it freely; what they cannot do is rest
exactly at it. Real things wobble within some neighbourhood
of the centre, drifting toward one Cardinal and a little
toward another as conditions push them about. The Equilibrium
is therefore best read as a region rather than a point — the
soft basin in the middle of the geometry into which ordinary
existence settles.

Mundus has one Equilibrium. Whether the same is true of any
other realm of existence — whether there is anywhere else
whose geometry has a centre to drift to at all — is not
something the foundation layer commits to.
