The **Nguwari** [[bay]] are a rare amphibious people of
[[realms/asthera|Asthera]] — serpentine in body, humanoid in
bearing — native to [[bayurinda|Bayurinda]]. Their Bayurindan
name glosses as _border swimmers_: the people who cross the
half-tide line, the seam between the archipelago's upper world
and [[nuunlau|Nuunlau]]. They are not of the deep, and they are
not of the surface alone; they are the kindred at the threshold
between the two.

The Nguwari share habitat with the archipelago's
[[kinds/human|human]] majority — present on the islands and
along the shores — but lean toward the tidal cave systems,
where the line between sea and stone is widest and a body that
moves freely between water and rock has the most room to live.

What general claims the compendium can make about the Nguwari
beyond this — their numbers, their distribution across
Bayurinda's archipelagos and cave-systems, their internal
divisions, languages, and broader relation to the Astheran
world, the part their bodies and languages play in the work
some among them are known for — wait on further entries.
