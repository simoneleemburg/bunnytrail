The axes of Mundus are the foundation-level structural quantities
that describe a thing's position in the interior of existence.

Mundus is bounded by three horizons — [[existence/source|the
Source]], [[existence/self|the Self]], and
[[existence/chaos|Chaos]]. Its interior is described, in
foundation-level terms, as a triangular region with the horizons
at the corners. A thing in Mundus has a single position in that
interior, and the natural way to read that position is by its
closeness to each of the three corners. Each closeness is an
axis.

The three axes are:

- **[[existence/resonance|Resonance]]**, defined by the Source.
- **[[existence/subjectivity|Subjectivity]]**, defined by the Self.
- **[[existence/dissolution|Dissolution]]**, defined by Chaos.

They are not independent. A thing has one position, not three;
the axis-readings sum to a constant, and moving toward one corner
necessarily moves the thing away from at least one of the
others.
