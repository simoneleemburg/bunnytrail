A realm of existence is not a place. It is one of the
underlying layers of which reality is composed — an axis along
which things exist, not a region they can be in.

Four such realms are currently registered: the Source, from
which selves arise; the Self, in which they narrate themselves;
Mundus, in which they are embodied; and Chaos, along which they
come apart. They are peers, not opposites. A coherent thing
participates in all of them at once.

Particular clusters have their own names for the realms, and
their own accounts of how the realms relate. Aurethia calls the
Source _Nareth_, the Self _Valdor_, Mundus _Asthera_. The
foundation layer prefers the vantage-neutral terms, and lets
each cluster's local account hold its own vocabulary.
