A realm of existence is a region of reality where massive,
persisting things exist as themselves.

It is what is bounded by the horizons. Mundus — the only
currently-registered realm — is the interior where bodies have
mass, identities persist, narratives continue, and matter
arranges itself into the configurations any cluster's physics
or cosmology describes from the inside.

The boundary against which a realm of existence is defined is
not a wall but a set of limits: directions along which things
can lean and approach, but cannot reach. The three horizons of
Mundus — the Source, the Self, and Chaos — are limits of this
kind.

Particular clusters name regions of Mundus in their own
vocabularies. They are all in Mundus; they differ in how close
they lean toward which horizon.
