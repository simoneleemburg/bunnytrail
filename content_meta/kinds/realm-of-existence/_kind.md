A realm of existence is a region of reality where massive,
persisting things exist as themselves.

It is what is bounded by the bounds of existence. Mundus — the
only currently-registered realm — is the interior where bodies
have mass, identities persist, narratives continue, and matter
arranges itself into the configurations any cluster's physics
or cosmology describes from the inside.

The boundary against which a realm of existence is defined is
not a wall but a set of [[kinds/bound|bounds]]: directions
along which things can lean and approach, but cannot reach.
The bounds of Mundus come in two shapes — three
[[kinds/cardinal|Cardinals]] at the corners and three
[[kinds/horizon|Horizons]] along the edges between them — both
asymptotic to mass.

Particular clusters name regions of Mundus in their own
vocabularies. They are all in Mundus; they differ in how close
they lean toward which bound.
